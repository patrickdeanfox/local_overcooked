## Dynamics
- Nothing moves. The obstacle is the corridor width.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's advice is a pipeline, not a split: "chefs should run circles in which they take Ingredients, then chop them and lastly put them in Pots", never turning back. Two chefs circling the same way never meet.
- Its alternative is to carry a pot to the chopping boards to shorten the run, which the mechanics allow.
- The bottleneck is walking, not cooking, so ticket cadence matters more here than the number of live tickets. The clone's playtest measured 60.9 s to a first soup and 47.7 s / 39.6 s after it when one chef does everything.
- Watch the burn clock: fetching, chopping and delivering one ingredient per lap needs 22-36 s for a full pot, and a pot burns 22 s after its first ingredient. The strategy that works is to park two chopped ingredients on the island counters at (7,5) and (8,5) and tip all three in together.
