# oc1 5-6 — Cosmic Canteen (four rooms, doors and conveyors)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-6_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/5-6.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **61 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 41.
  Player counts 1-4; the Strategies suggest four controllers with two chefs parked on the plates.
- Recipes: Onion Soup, Tomato Soup, Mushroom Soup — 3 order variants. Cadence not published:
  initial 2, one new order every 24 s, max 4 on screen, 90 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 40/80/140/240 (1P), 40/100/180/300 (2P),
  60/140/220/360 (3P), 80/200/320/520 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 20/60/100/160 (1P),
  40/80/140/240 (2P), 40/100/160/260 (3P), 60/140/240/380 (4P). The wiki explains why they are so
  low: "the star milestones are very generous due to the kitchen being complicated". The JSON carries
  the AYCE-Switch numbers, **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: sink kitchen. 3 plates, and they start in the **lower-right** room; the sink is in the
  **lower-left**; the plate return is in the **upper-left**. No single room can complete the plate
  cycle.

## Dynamics
- **Two pressure-plate doors, cross-wired.** Wiki: the lower-left plate "opens a passage between the
  two upper sections"; the upper-right plate "allows for opening the lower door". Held while a chef
  stands on the plate `(estimate — same reasoning as 5-3)`.
- **Two one-way conveyor belts.** Upper-left sends down to lower-left; lower-right sends up to
  upper-right. Belt speed is not published — ~1.5 tiles/s `(estimate)`. Unlike 4-4's belt, these do
  **not** end in a bin: they deliver into the receiving room.
- Nothing else moves; no fall hazard.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies: "Instead of sending single ingredients down to the lower-left section, grab a Pot
  and throw all of the Ingredients in before sending it back" — the belt carries **cookware**, not
  just ingredients, and batching a whole pot is the intended play.
- The circulation is a one-way loop: raw ingredients start on the divider in the lower rooms, chopped
  ingredients have to reach the boards in the upper-right, cooked soup has to reach the serving
  counter in the upper-left, and dirty plates have to reach the sink in the lower-left. Every stage
  crosses a door or a belt.
- The bottleneck is that one chef must stand on a plate for the other to move, exactly as in 5-3,
  but here with a belt in each direction as well.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Pressure plates bound to doors** (shared with 5-3), cross-wired between rooms.
2. **Conveyor belts** that carry cookware as well as ingredients and deliver into a room (no bin at
   the end) — a different case from 4-4's belt-into-a-bin.
3. Four independent rooms in one kitchen, with the serving counter, sink, plate return and plates all
   in different ones.
4. No new recipes or stations otherwise — three soups, two pot burners, two boards, a sink, a bin and
   an extinguisher are all in the clone already.
