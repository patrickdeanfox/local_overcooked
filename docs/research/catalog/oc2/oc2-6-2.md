# oc2 6-2 — Gourmet Galaxy
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/6-2_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/6-2.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 70 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (5 order variants): Pancake, Chocolate Pancake, Cake, Carrot Cake, Chocolate Cake.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 100 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 160/200/280 · 2P 180/220/300 · 3P 200/240/340 · 4P 200/240/340. The 4★ bonus tier is 600 / 650 / 720 / 800 — the lowest 4★ bar in world 6.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 3 plates. Plate count is not on the wiki; three is an estimate. All chefs start on the left island, which also holds the sink, so plates start there. The plate return is on the top island.

## Dynamics
- **sinking-platforms** (18 s) — platforms surface for about 13 s and sink for about 5 s, cycling through a fixed sequence of shapes from wide land to narrow paths.
- **Hazards** — water: anything carried is lost, chefs respawn; bowls dropped in water respawn at their stand after 5 s.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's split is by route, not by task: one chef runs left↔top, the other left↔right.
- The bottleneck is the ~13 s window a platform is up. There is barely time to cross and come back, so the wiki's technique is to throw from halfway across and to move 2–3 items per trip.
- Priority rule from the wiki: a chef carrying cake mix or a finished dish takes the narrow path first, because that cargo is lost if they do not make it.
- Deliberate loss is a tactic — drop bowls into the water so they respawn at their stand after 5 s instead of walking them back.
- Chefs can also throw to each other diagonally from the top island to the left one by both standing at the edge, facing each other.
