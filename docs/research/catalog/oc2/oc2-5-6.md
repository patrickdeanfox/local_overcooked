## Facts
- **Time** 5:10 (310 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 62 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (4 order variants): Beef Pasta, Mushroom Pasta, Tomato Pasta, Fish & Prawn Pasta.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 25 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 280/360/480 · 2P 300/380/520 · 3P 320/400/500 · 4P 320/400/500. The 4★ bonus tier is 950 / 1540 / 1600 / 1700.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 3 plates. The wiki is explicit: the three plates start on the bottom-right island with the sink, the pans and the boards — the island that also has no serve counter.

## Dynamics
- **phases** (period unknown) — phase 2 begins at 3:48 remaining (the river becomes solid ground and counters start sliding to new places); phase 3 begins at 1:58 remaining (a left/right split with an extra pot and burner, new fish and prawn crates, a ledge you drop off and a portal back).
- **moving-platforms** (period unknown) — phase 1: small land bridges drift between the islands and pause briefly when it is safe to cross.
- **sliders** (period unknown) — phase 2: counters split and slide across the kitchen into their phase-3 positions.
- **portal** (period unknown) — phase 3 only: a portal returns you from the right area to the left.
- **Hazards** — river (phase 1); the ledge and the void (phase 3).

## Cooperation notes
- Phase 1: the left chef starts pasta and throws sauce ingredients right, then walks a pot over to plate it — the wiki warns the crossing is tight and it is often cheaper to walk into the river and respawn than to attempt the return trip.
- The wiki notes the connecting platforms briefly pause when it is safe to cross, even when they do not look joined.
- Phase 3 has its own throwing rule: aiming down or down-right at the ledge overshoots out of bounds, so throw flat to the right or simply drop items over the ledge.
- Phase 3 also adds Fish & Prawn Pasta, a variant that exists nowhere else in the level, and forces a full lap of the kitchen per dish — so all chefs share duties with perhaps one staying permanently on the right.
