# oc2 5-2 — Buffet Balloons
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-2_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/5-2.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 47 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (4 order variants): Meat Burger, Cheese Burger, Lettuce & Cheese Burger, Tomato & Lettuce Burger.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 180/220/300 · 2P 240/300/420 · 3P 200/240/340 · 4P 200/240/340. The 4★ bonus tier is 580 / 1100 / 1250 / 1550. The 3- and 4-player 1★–3★ thresholds (200/240/340) are *lower* than the 2-player ones (240/300/420).
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 4 plates. Plate count is not on the wiki; four is read from the round discs along the bottom counter of the ring corridor. The plate return is in the right station and the sink sits on the wall the two stations share.

## Dynamics

- **Hazards** — random floor fire.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's split: the right chef builds burgers and ferries dirty plates to the sink; the left chef passes cheese and tomato right, washes and serves.
- The bottleneck is the serve counter — it is on the far left of the map, off the ring corridor, so every finished burger walks the whole loop.
- Fires start at random and block corridors, and the wiki warns they can corner a chef, so someone must always be within reach of the extinguisher (which lives in the right station, away from the bin).
