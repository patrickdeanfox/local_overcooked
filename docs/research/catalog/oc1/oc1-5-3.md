# oc1 5-3 — Cosmic Canteen (pressure plates)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-3_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/5-3.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **51 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 32.
  Player counts 1-4; the Strategies note that at 4 players two chefs can simply park on the plates.
- Recipes: Beef Burrito, Chicken Burrito — 2 order variants. Cadence not published: initial 2, one
  new order every 24 s, max 4 on screen, 90 s each `(estimate)` — long, because the plate cycle
  makes every dish slow.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 160/340/540/840 (1P), 180/420/660/1020 (2P),
  220/500/780/1220 (3P), 260/560/880/1360 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 80/180/300/480 (1P),
  120/260/400/620 (2P), 220/500/780/1220 (3P), 280/600/940/1460 (4P). The JSON carries the
  AYCE-Switch numbers, **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: **no sink** (`plates=No`). Dirty plates come back clean at the plate return, which the wiki
  calls out as making the level easier. 3 plates, starting on the north wall in the upper-left.

## Layout
- Grid size: **15 x 9**, counted from `5-3.jpg`; confidence **med**. Method: the south wall reads
  `pan, counter, pot, counter, pan, counter, pot` at ~53 px per tile, which fixes the left half at
  7 columns; the vertical divider and the three crates east of it add 7 more. Rows come from the two
  floor bands (3 rows each) plus three wall/divider rows.

- Legend extensions used: `?` **pressure plate** (a floor panel that opens a door while a chef
  stands on it), `G` **door** in the vertical divider, walkable only while the *other* half's plate
  is held down, `I` rice crate, `C` chicken crate, `!` tortilla crate.
- Station inventory: boards 2 (both in the upper-right), **pot burners 2**, **pan burners 2** (all
  four on the south wall), fryers 0, ovens 0, mixers 0, steamers 0, **sinks 0**, serve counters 1,
  plate returns 1, bins 0, extinguishers 1, plates 3, pressure plates 2, doors 2, crates 4 (rice on
  the north wall; tortilla, chicken and beef on the south wall), plain counters 45. Floor 72,
  counter 63, station 18.
- Spawns: the capture puts one chef in the upper-left at (4,1) and one in the lower-left at (3,5)
  `(estimate)`.
- Zones — four rooms, cross-wired:
  - **Upper-left** (cols 1-7, rows 1-3): serving counter, plate return, three plates, the rice crate,
    and the **upper pressure plate** at (6,2).
  - **Upper-right** (cols 9-13, rows 1-3): the two chopping boards. Reached only through the door at
    (8,2), which the *lower* chef's plate opens.
  - **Lower-left** (cols 1-7, rows 5-7): the four burners and the extinguisher on the south and west
    walls, and the **lower pressure plate** at (6,6).
  - **Lower-right** (cols 9-13, rows 5-7): tortilla, chicken and beef crates. Reached only through
    the door at (8,6), which the *upper* chef's plate opens.
  - Upper and lower halves are **permanently separated** by the counter run at row 4; everything
    passes over it. Only the rice crate can be walked from crate to serving counter (5 tiles); the
    other three crates are on the wrong side of a wall that never opens.

## Dynamics
- **Two pressure plates, cross-wired.** Standing on the plate in one half opens the door in the
  *other* half. Wiki: "they allow chefs to open passageways for each other to reach the right side of
  the kitchen (the upper chef(s) get access to Chopping Boards and the lower chef(s) get access to
  every ingredient except for Rice)". The Strategies section spells out the intended cycle, which
  confirms the wiring: upper chef stands → lower chef fetches meat; lower chef stands → upper chef
  goes to chop.
- The doors are **held open**, not timed: they open while weight is on the plate and shut when the
  chef steps off `(estimate — the wiki does not state it, but the whole strategy cycle only works
  this way)`. No published open/close animation time.
- Nothing else moves. No fall hazard, no conveyor, no cannon.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki gives a full eight-step loop, which is unusual and worth copying verbatim into a level
  script: upper stands → lower fetches beef and chicken to the shared right counters and carries
  tortillas across → lower stands → upper drops rice on the shared left counters and crosses to chop
  meat → both cook simultaneously (lower boils rice, upper chops) → lower stands again to release the
  choppers → lower fries the meat → upper assembles and serves.
- The bottleneck is that **one chef is always standing still**. With two players the kitchen runs at
  roughly half staff at all times; with four, two chefs are pure doormen.
- The serving counter is in the upper half only, so every finished burrito ends its life crossing the
  central counter run northward.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Pressure plates and doors** — a floor tile that is "occupied by a chef" and a gate tile bound to
   it. The clone has `gate` tiles but they open on a timer, not on a trigger; this needs a
   plate → gate-group binding, and cross-wiring between two of them.
2. **Burrito recipe family** and the rice / tortilla / chicken crates (shared with 5-1 and 5-2).
3. A **sink-less kitchen** where dirty plates return clean — the clone's `plates.mode: 'stack'` is
   the nearest thing but respawns at a stack tile rather than at the plate return.
4. Nothing else: burners, boards, counters and a serving counter are all present.
