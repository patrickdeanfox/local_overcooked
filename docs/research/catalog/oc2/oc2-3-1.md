# oc2 3-1 — Conjurer's Kitchen
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-1_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 3-1.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 3:30 (210 s). **Delayed timer** — the wiki says the clock does not start until the first pizza is served, so the intended opening is to bake seven or eight pizzas onto spare counter space first.
- Unlock: 16 stars. Playable 1–4 players. Scoring 600 or more unlocks the hidden level Kevin 4.
- Recipes: Pizza only, 1 order variant (dough + cheese + tomato, all chopped, assembled, baked). First pizza level and first oven.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~26 s, max 4, ~100 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Three plates, starting on the bottom left with the tomato crate and the extinguisher.
- Obstacles: moving tables, isolated chefs; infobox `dynamic = Yes`.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 240/340/460/830; 2P 480/600/820/1500; 3P 560/780/1040/1650; 4P 560/780/1040/1900. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **sliders** — the counter carrying the three chopping boards moves left or right about every 30 seconds, swapping which chef can reach the stairs, ovens and sink. Period 30 s.
- Hazard: oven fire if a pizza is left baking

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's whole strategy is the delayed timer: pre-bake a wall of pizzas, then spend the clocked portion of the level washing plates and serving the stockpile.
- Bottleneck: one staircase, and only one chef can reach it at a time. Roles have to swap every time the counter moves, which is what makes this an "isolated chefs" level.
- The 2P 1★ threshold (480) is double the 1P one (240) — the level expects the pre-bake trick.

## Recreate checklist
- Oven station and the pizza recipe (chop several ingredients, assemble onto the dough, bake, plate) — the assembly is carried by the dough, not by the plate.
- A raised area joined by stairs (a one-tile chokepoint the clone can already express as floor).
- A sliding counter group that acts as a moving *wall*, not just a moving surface — the clone's `sliders` dynamic moves counters but the levels so far never cut the walkable graph with one.
- New ingredients: dough, cheese.
