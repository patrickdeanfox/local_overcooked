# -- Tutorials (docs/MECHANICS.md, the tutorial kitchens under src/levels/tutorial/): the Tutorials

#    tray tutorial, every step of it walked with the pointer over the tile it names, the closing
#    banner, then a one-player chop assist run skipped from the panel (the prep hint comes back), and
