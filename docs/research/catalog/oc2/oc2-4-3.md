# oc2 4-3 — Moreish Mines
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-3_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/4-3.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 32 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (3 order variants): Meat Burger, Cheese Burger, Lettuce & Cheese Burger.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 180/220/300 · 2P 200/240/340 · 3P 300/380/520 · 4P 300/380/520. The 4★ bonus tier is 600 / 950 / 1100 / 1300. Earning a x4 tip combo here unlocks the hidden level Kevin 5.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 3 plates. All three plates start on the left half's top counter, next to the bun and cheese crates. The plate return is on the right half, so a finished plate goes right-to-left and a dirty plate goes left-to-right.

## Dynamics
- **conveyors** (30 s) — the two S-shaped belts swap direction every 30 seconds.
- **Hazards** — none.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's split: the left chef throws ingredients right, washes plates and serves; the right chef chops and cooks.
- The bottleneck is the single counter tile on the dividing wall — the wiki says to reserve it for dirty dishes going left and finished patties going right, not for raw ingredients (those get thrown).
- The wiki also advises the right chef to ignore the belts entirely and walk hugging the counter corner between the pans and the boards, because a belt that reverses under you costs more than the shortcut saves.
