## Dynamics
- **belt-and-button** (period unknown) — ingredients ride belts into bins; pressing a side's button diverts that side's two ingredients into reach until it pops back up. Eggs are the only ingredient grabbable without a press, right before they drop.
- **Hazards** — ingredients are binned if nobody presses the button; central pit.

## Cooperation notes
- One chef is effectively on button duty while the other stockpiles: the wiki's opening advice is to press both buttons and keep re-pressing them as soon as they pop back up.
- The bottleneck is ingredient access itself. Nothing is picked up from a crate — every ingredient rides a belt into a bin and is only reachable while its button is held down. Eggs are the single exception: they can be grabbed just before they drop.
- Because the left button covers flour and chocolate and the right covers eggs and honey, and a cake needs three of those four, the two chefs cannot work independently.
