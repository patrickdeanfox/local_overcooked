# oc2 5-5 — Conjurer's Kitchen
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-5_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/5-5.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 3:30 (210 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 58 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (3 order variants): Beef Pasta, Mushroom Pasta, Tomato Pasta.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 160/200/280 · 2P 280/360/480 · 3P 280/360/480 · 4P 280/360/480. The 4★ bonus tier is 650 / 1000 / 1050 / 1300. Earning a x5 tip combo here unlocks Kevin 7.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 3 plates. The wiki is explicit: the three plates start in the top-right corner with the three pots. The sink is on the right wall between the two right corners and the plate return is in the bottom-right corner.

## Dynamics
- **cross-controlled-platforms** (period unknown) — each half's small platform is driven only by the switches in the other half's two corners; the useful stops are in front of the sink / boards and up beside the cooking stations.
- **Hazards** — the rift: walk in and respawn.

## Cooperation notes
- This is the game's clearest 'you cannot help yourself, only your partner' level: the switches in the two left corners drive the right-hand platform and vice versa.
- The wiki says the first thing both chefs should do is move their partner's platform so it connects their top and bottom corners, and that there are only two useful stops — in front of the sink/boards, and up beside the cooking stations.
- The split: the left chef makes sauces and serves; the right chef boils the pasta, throws mushrooms across, washes and passes plated pasta to the middle counter.
- Two efficiency notes from the wiki: bring the plate to the pan rather than the pan to the plate, and stagger three pots so you can wash between them (the pots beep but do not burn if you time it).
