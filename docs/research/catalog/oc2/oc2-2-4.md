# oc2 2-4 — Moreish Mines
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/2-4_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 2-4.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00 (240 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 10 stars. Playable 1–4 players. The level nav lists Kevin 3 as the hidden level from here.
- Recipes: Beef Burrito and Mushroom Burrito, 2 order variants. First burrito level.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~26 s, max 4, ~100 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Four plates — three start on the left side, one on the right.
- Obstacle: bottleneck (infobox) — a single bridge across a mine chasm.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 300/420/560/1250; 2P 360/480/660/1700; 3P 500/700/940/2500; 4P 500/700/940/2600. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

- Every burrito needs boiled rice (rice crate on the right, pots on the left) *and* fried chopped meat (meat crate and pans on the right, plate on the left), so the bridge carries traffic in both directions on every single dish.

## Dynamics
- Hazard: fall into the chasm: chef respawns (delay not on the wiki)
- Hazard: pan fire

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki roles: left chef puts tortillas on plates, throws mushrooms across and does the dishes while rice boils; right chef chops and fries and throws rice across.
- Bottleneck: the one-tile bridge. The wiki's answer is to throw over it rather than queue on it.
- The 3P/4P star thresholds jump sharply (940 for 3★ against 660 at 2P), so the level expects extra chefs to parallelise the chop/fry chain rather than crowd the bridge.
