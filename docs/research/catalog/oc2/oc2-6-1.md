# oc2 6-1 — Buffet Balloons
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/6-1_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/6-1.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts on the first serve — the wiki says so explicitly, this is the only level in worlds 4–6 with prep time. **Unlock** 66 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (3 order variants): Cake, Carrot Cake, Chocolate Cake.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 100 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 360/460/620 · 2P 420/520/700 · 3P 580/720/980 · 4P 580/720/980. The 4★ bonus tier is 820 / 1250 / 1100 / 1450. Note the 4-player 3★ (980) is higher than the 1-player 4★ (820) — the cake chain scales badly with one chef.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 3 plates. The wiki is explicit: the three plates start in the middle band with the two boards and the extinguisher. The plate return is in the top band and the sink in the bottom band, so a plate's round trip crosses both walls.

## Dynamics
- **conveyors** (period unknown) — belts down the left wall carry ingredients between the three bands; the walls are too high to throw over, so the belts and the right-hand corridor are the only routes.
- **fire** (period unknown) — floor fires start at random points and block access.
- **Hazards** — random floor fire.

## Cooperation notes
- This is the level where the timer does not start until the first dish is served — the wiki says so — so the opening is a free prep window. Every cake needs honey, so chop honey first.
- The wiki warns of a cap of five baked cakes waiting at once, and that over-prepping makes surplus ingredients despawn.
- The bottleneck is that the walls are too high to throw over. The belts and the right-hand corridor are the only routes, which makes a dedicated chopper worthwhile only when you have enough players; the wiki says two chefs should ignore the belts.
- Random floor fires block access on top of that, so the extinguisher (in the middle band, with the plates) gets used constantly.
