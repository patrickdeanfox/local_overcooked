# oc1 1-6 — Treacle Town
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-6_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/1-6.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00, no prep time. Unlock: 8 stars (infobox and table agree). 1-4 players. The only Overcooked! kitchen with an elevated half.
- The three burgers again. Three variants. No cadence on the wiki; the clone runs initial 2, every 24 s, max 4, 100 s each — slower than 1-4 because the seam is shut most of the time and every burger crosses it.
- OC1 columns blank on the level page, but the Level 1 summary fills in 1P **40 / 60 / 90**. Only the 2P 3-star (200) exists elsewhere; scaling the 1P row by 200/90 would put a two-player 1-star at 90, as demanding as the whole one-player 3-star run, so the clone uses 30% / 60% of the 3-star instead: [60, 120, 200]. Provenance `oc1` for 1P and the 2P 3-star.
- `plates = Yes`: sink (2,0) with rack (3,0) on the low side, plate return (12,5) on the high side. Three clean plates on the high side's north wall at (7,0), (9,0), (11,0).

## Dynamics
- "A constant earthquake that splits the right and left sides of the kitchen. The right side is elevated, meaning all chefs can drop down at any moment." No period on the wiki, so the timing is `(estimate)`.
- The clone models the seam as a gate open 4 s in every 10, starting open. That is symmetric and the original is not: in the real 1-6 the high side can always be left by dropping down, and everything else is thrown across. This is the largest approximation in the shipped world.
- The clone's playtest confirmed the seam shoves rather than swallows: a chef standing at x=6.4 when the gate shuts is put down at x=5.7, flush against the seam on the side it came from.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki gives the whole plan: "have one chef chop ingredients, and when the floor lowers chuck all of the food to the right", plus "be prepared to wash dishes in the Sink as you have limited time to do so and to throw them to the upper level".
- So the low chef is a prep and dishwashing station and the high chef is a cook and server, and the seam is a batching problem: build a pile, cross once.
- 1-6 has no equivalent of 1-4's divider hand-off. The two halves only meet on the seam, so every part is carried across during an open window.

## Recreate checklist
- Throwing, if the level is to play like the original rather than like a timed gate. The clone has neither throwing nor one-way tiles.
- One-way vertical movement (drop down from the high side, climb back only when level).
- Otherwise nothing: shipped in `src/levels/oc1/1-6.json` as a symmetric gate.
