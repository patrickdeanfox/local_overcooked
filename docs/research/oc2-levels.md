# Overcooked! 2 — level catalogue

Every base-campaign level (1-1 … 6-6) plus the eight hidden Kevin levels. All fields are transcribed
from the level's wiki infobox and Overview section; star thresholds come from each page's Star Chart.
Screenshots are in `screens/oc2/`.

DLC campaigns are listed by name and level count only (§4).

## 1. Level table

Notes on the columns:

- **Recipes** — recipe family, with the number of distinct *order variants* the level can ask for in
  brackets. A "variant" is one row of the level's Recipes section, e.g. Fish Sashimi vs Prawn Sashimi.
- **Plates** — `sink` = the level has a sink and plates return dirty; `no sink` = plates return clean.
  This is the wiki infobox's `plates` field.
- **Gimmick** — the infobox `obstacles` field, prefixed **dynamic** where the infobox `dynamic` field
  is Yes (the level's layout changes mid-run).
- **Star (2P)** — 1★/2★/3★/4★ score thresholds for two players. Every level also has separate 1P, 3P
  and 4P charts; see the wiki page.

## 2. Layout and cooperation, one line per level

### World 1 — Sushi City / Buffet Balloons

**1-1** Open square room; fish box mid-left, shrimp box mid-right, four chopping boards in the bottom
two corners, four plates on two centre islands, plate return next to the serving counter. Cooperation
is thin by design — the only pressure is that both crates and both board clusters are at opposite ends,
so chefs naturally split left/right and the plates should never leave the return.

**1-2** Three ingredient boxes in the middle, three boards top-left, stove bottom-right, serve + plate
return top-right, bin bottom-middle. First sushi level: the boiled-rice chain and the chop-fish chain
run at different speeds, so one chef owns the pot and one owns the plate, and pedestrians cutting
through the room force you to time the hand-off.

**1-3** Shrimp/nori/rice boxes mid-left, cucumber mid-right, three boards top-left, stove top-right,
serve bottom-left, sink + plate return bottom-right. The wiki's own split is "left chef chops, plates
and serves; right chef washes and cooks rice" — the level is built as a diagonal, so every dish needs
one item from the other chef's half.

**1-4** All four boxes top-left, two boards top-right, three stoves middle- and bottom-right, sink and
bin middle-left, serve + plate return bottom-middle, and two horizontal conveyor belts across the top
and bottom. The belts make walking against them costly, so the intended loop is one-directional: the
rice cook rides the belt with the flow and the chopper works against it only when the cook is clear.

**1-5** Two hot-air balloons. The big left balloon has two pans and two pots plus a *sliding* three-board
counter and the bin; the small right balloon has the sink, both ingredient boxes, the plate return and
the serve window. The chefs are physically specialised: the small-balloon chef can only fetch, wash and
serve, so nothing gets cooked unless they keep feeding the cook.

**1-6** *Dynamic.* Phase 1 is a square balloon: serve + return on top, two boards and a plate on the
left, two plates + sink + bin on the bottom, three boxes on the right, and a central flame that shoots
floor fire to random tiles. At 2:20 the balloon crashes into a sushi restaurant and the whole square
becomes the *centre* of a much bigger kitchen with three ingredient-feeding conveyors, two more boards,
a second sink, a second bin and two pots. Cooperation comes from the recipe mix (salad and sushi at
once) plus fire cutting one chef off from the extinguisher.

### World 2 — Ravenous Rapids / Buffet Balloons / Moreish Mines

**2-1** Two rafts. The top raft has nothing but two chopping boards; the bottom raft has everything else
(two deep fryers, chicken and potato boxes, serve, return, bin, extinguisher, four plates). This is the
purest throwing level in the game: the top chef cannot cook or serve, so their only output is chopped
food thrown down, and the bottom chef must throw raw food up.

**2-2** Three balloons in a line. Left balloon: serve counter plus beef and noodle boxes. Middle balloon:
shrimp and fish boxes and two boards — and it *moves up and down*, so it is only sometimes reachable.
Right balloon: a square of four burners (two pots, two pans), plates, bin, extinguisher, return. The
middle balloon is the bottleneck; the wiki's advice is to throw beef and pasta straight to the right
balloon rather than wait for the ferry.

**2-3** Three balloons: left has sink, return, serve, one board and all three boxes; right has a board,
bin, four plates, two pans, two pots and the extinguisher; a fourth balloon of four counters drifts
back and forth underneath as a conveyor. Left chops and throws across, right cooks — and the drifting
counters are the plate ferry.

**2-4** Two sides split by a bridge. Left: serve, return, two pots, tortilla and mushroom boxes. Right:
two boards, sink, two pans, bin, extinguisher, beef and rice boxes. Every burrito needs boiled rice
(left) *and* fried chopped meat (right), so the bridge is a permanent two-way traffic problem; the
canonical split is "left plates and washes, right chops and fries, both throw".

**2-5** Left and right halves joined by a middle rail, with four ingredient boxes riding a counter that
slides left and right along that rail — so which crate you can reach changes constantly. Left has two
pots, the sink, three boards and the extinguisher; right has serve, return, bin and four pans. Because
crates come and go, the wiki's advice is to over-chop while a crate is in reach and throw the surplus.

**2-6** A cross-shaped counter at the centre holds three pans and the four plates, and **rotates
counter-clockwise every 30 seconds**, repeatedly cutting the room in half along a different axis. Boxes
(bun, beef, cheese) top-left, sink middle-left, two boards + bin + extinguisher top-right, serve +
return bottom-left. You must keep one chef on each side or someone is stranded from the serve window.

### World 3 — Conjurer's Kitchen / Ravenous Rapids

**3-1** *Dynamic.* Raised top section holds serve, return and bin, reached by stairs. Bottom-left has
the extinguisher, tomato box and three plates; bottom-right has two ovens, the sink, dough and cheese.
Between them a three-board counter slides left or right every ~30 s, **changing which chef can reach the
stairs, the ovens and the sink** — so roles have to swap on a timer.

**3-2** Three tiers: left (bun and lettuce boxes, three boards), middle-lower (cheese and beef boxes,
four pans), right (serve, return, bin, extinguisher). Middle connects to left by stairs at the start,
but the stairs move every 35 s; left and right are *always* connected by portals, and there is a second
portal pair up top that items can be thrown through. The intended play is one chef parked in the middle
cooking patties while the other chops and throws down.

**3-3** Four quadrants with ingredient boxes (dough, pepperoni, tomato, cheese) that physically slide to
wall the quadrants off — all open for the first 10 s, then a vertical split, alternating with a
horizontal split every 30–40 s. Bin + board top-left, board + extinguisher + serve top-right, oven +
return bottom-right, oven + sink bottom-left. Keeping one clean plate on the middle counter is the trick
that keeps both chefs able to finish a pizza whichever way the walls fall.

**3-4** Three sections: a raised middle with serve and return, reached from either side only through
portals, and left and right sides separated by **an uncrossable rift you can only throw across**. Left
has two boards, three pans and the extinguisher; right has the sink, bin and all five boxes. The right
chef is a supplier who cannot cook and the left chef is a cook with no crates.

**3-5** Two drifting rafts. Left: serve, return, two deep fryers, potato and chicken boxes. Right: two
boards, sink, bin, extinguisher, both plates. The rafts periodically drift too far apart to walk
between, so throwing is not an optimisation, it is the transport layer — and the wiki says always throw
extra.

**3-6** *Dynamic.* Three platforms: top (two boards, two pans, extinguisher, serve), bottom (two pots,
bin, return), and a middle platform carrying **all four ingredient boxes** that swings between them. At
2:20 the mine floods and all three become drifting rafts. Nobody has a full recipe on their own
platform at any point; the middle is the only crate access and it is always leaving.

### World 4 — Sushi City / Ravenous Rapids / Moreish Mines

**4-1** Left and right kitchens split by a **road with cars** that kill chefs when the light is green.
Left: two pots, two boards, sink, cucumber box, and a conveyor that spawns rice and nori. Right: serve,
return, extinguisher, bin, and a conveyor spawning prawn and fish. The road makes walking across a risk
you take rarely — so seafood gets thrown left and plating happens right.

**4-2** Two rafts that alternate between being connected vertically and horizontally. Each has an
L-shaped conveyor ending in a bin, and when the rafts line up, dropping a plate on that belt is the
*only* way to move a plate across. Top raft: two fryers, sink, board, tomato and lettuce boxes. Bottom
raft: serve, return, extinguisher, two boards, potato and chicken boxes. Plates cannot be thrown, so
plate logistics and ingredient logistics use two different channels.

**4-3** Left and right joined by S-shaped conveyors that **reverse every 30 s**. Left: serve, sink, four
boxes, three plates. Right: two pans, two boards, bin, return, extinguisher. A single counter tile
between the halves is the manual hand-off; everything else has to ride a belt that may be pointing the
wrong way.

**4-4** Left and right joined by top and bottom bridges, with **gusts of wind along both bridges that
flip direction every ~13 s and always oppose each other** — one bridge is always a headwind. Each side
has a board, a fryer and one crate (chicken left, potato right); the sink is left, serve/return/
extinguisher right. Neither chef can make a Nuggets-and-Chips order alone.

**4-5** Top and bottom halves ringed by an outer conveyor, with two overlapping L-shaped belts in the
middle that both end in bins — **buttons on each side redirect the middle belts toward their own half**.
Top: two pots, serve, return, nori and rice. Bottom: two boards, sink, extinguisher, fish and cucumber.
Only two plates, and plates cannot be thrown, so the belt-plus-button handshake is the whole level.

**4-6** Left and right joined by **walkable conveyor floors** (top belt runs right, bottom belt runs
left). The outer walls carry counters that swap what they hold every 30 s — left starts with rice,
chicken and mushroom crates, right with two boards plus tortilla and beef. Left has sink, bin, two pans;
right has two pots, serve, return. The wiki's recommended two-player strategy is deliberately
*low*-coordination: each chef owns whole orders end-to-end and only the crate rotation forces contact.

### World 5 — mixed

**5-1** Left and right banks with a **player-steered platform** bridging the river, carrying the three
plates. Left: two pots, two pans, bin, bun/nori/rice boxes. Right: serve, return, sink, two boards,
extinguisher, cucumber/beef/fish boxes. One chef drives the ferry, and the ferry is where clean plates
live, so the driver controls everyone's tempo.

**5-2** Two work stations joined by a ring corridor, with the serve counter far left. Left station: one
pan, a board, bin, cheese and tomato. Right station: two pans, a board, lettuce and beef, return,
extinguisher. They *share* the sink and the bun crate. **Random floor fires** block the corridors and
can corner a chef, so someone must always be near the extinguisher.

**5-3** The chefs stand on a **steerable platform inside a pit**, surrounded by a ring of stations they
can only reach by driving to them: three pots bottom-left, three pans bottom-right, five crates on the
left wall, sink top-left, three boards top-right, serve/return/bin/extinguisher on the right wall. The
whole level is a shared-vehicle problem — you cannot be at the boards and the pots at once.

**5-4** Three sections. The bottom connects to the two top sections by stairs that **disappear and are
replaced by portals every ~35 s**; a third portal floats left and right over the top sections and
determines where the bottom portal spits you out — sometimes into the pit. Top-left: sink, return,
chocolate, two plates. Top-right: flour, two boards, serve. Bottom: two mixers, two pans, egg box, bin,
extinguisher. Chocolate is thrown top-left → top-right and flour is thrown down.

**5-5** Four corners split by one long central counter, plus **two small platforms where each chef's
switches move the *other* chef's platform**. Top-left: serve + three pans. Top-right: three pots + three
plates. Bottom-right: return, bin, mushroom and pasta. Bottom-left: bin, extinguisher, beef and tomato.
Two boards sit on the left middle. It is the game's clearest "you cannot help yourself, only your
partner" level.

**5-6** *Dynamic, three phases.* Phase 1: three islands (left = two pots + four crates; bottom-right =
two pans, two boards, sink, three plates; top-right = serve, return, bin, extinguisher) crossable only
when drifting land bridges line up. At 3:48 remaining the river becomes solid ground and counters begin
sliding to new positions. At 1:58 remaining the kitchen settles into a left/right split with an extra
pot and burner, extra fish and prawn crates, and a new Fish-and-Prawn Pasta order that exists only in
this phase. Throwing across the ledge overshoots, so late-phase transfer means throwing flat or dropping.

### World 6 — Gourmet Galaxy / Buffet Balloons

**6-1** Three work areas (top: two ovens, return, bin, flour and chocolate; bottom: two mixers, sink,
egg/carrot/honey; middle: two boards, extinguisher, three plates) joined by conveyors on the left and a
corridor on the right, with the serve counter far right. **The walls are high enough that you cannot
throw into the middle** — the belts and the corridor are the only routes, which is unusual for OC2 and
makes the chopper a dedicated role. Random floor fires on top of that.

**6-2** Three islands joined by platforms that surface for ~13 s and sink for ~5 s, cycling through a
fixed sequence of shapes from wide land to narrow paths. Left (start): two mixers, two pans, two boards,
sink, extinguisher. Right: serve, bin, egg and chocolate. Top: two ovens, return, flour/honey/carrot.
Nothing survives a missed crossing, so the wiki's technique is to throw from halfway across and to drop
bowls in the water deliberately so they respawn at their stand.

**6-3** Conveyors carry every ingredient toward bins; **a button redirects them into reach** — left
button for flour and chocolate, right for eggs and honey — and eggs are the only ingredient grabbable
without a press. Two boards bottom-left, two mixers + extinguisher top-left, two ovens + sink top-right,
two pans + serve + return bottom-right. One chef is effectively on button duty while the other stockpiles.

**6-4** A floating balloon platform with **two control sticks that drive two 2×2 ingredient-box islands**
around it. Utensils sit in the middle, sink on the right. Left island: cheese, bun, beef, dough. Right
island: lettuce, tomato, pepperoni, chicken. With burgers and pizzas ordered together you have to read
the ticket list and pre-position the islands, which is a purely communicative task.

**6-5** Top and bottom halves linked only by a circular conveyor in the middle; each half has left and
right sections that **sink alternately every 45 s**, telegraphed by a rumble. Each left has two pans and
bun/cheese/beef/lettuce; each right has two boards and a bin. Top has serve and return; bottom has the
sink, extinguisher and the three plates. Items left in a sinking section survive, but pans keep burning
and the fires go out underwater.

**6-6** *Dynamic finale, 15:00.* You cook two of every dish in the game in a fixed scripted order. The
serve counter and plate return start behind a **gate that a button on either side opens**. The first
half needs chopping, pots, pans and deep frying; after the burgers are delivered the layout switches to
a portal-and-conveyor kitchen for mixing and baking, and the ground breaks after the last burger. Items
can be thrown through the portals.

### Kevin levels (all Steamed Food / dumplings)

**Kevin 1** Two mirrored sides, one chef each, three tiers per side (plate stuff on top, boards in the
middle, crates at the bottom). Left has two mixers and beef; right has two steamers, flour and fish, plus
serve and return; sink is on the left. Fish-only dumplings skip the mixer, everything else needs one — so
the right side must throw flour left and the left side must feed mixes back.

**Kevin 2** Two centre counters ringed by four 3×3 squares that get **pushed clockwise then
counter-clockwise every ~23 s starting at 3:00 remaining**. Top-left: two steamers, extinguisher, sink.
Top-right: return, a steamer, prawn and flour. Bottom-left: bin, fish/carrot/beef. Bottom-right: three
boards, two mixers. One plate per square, so which chef owns which station rotates under you.

**Kevin 3** Lower level (two mixers, serve, return, extinguisher, beef/carrot/fish), a top high level
(three steamers, sink, bin, flour and prawn) and a right high level (two boards) — linked only by **one
elevator**. The wiki's note is to keep a dedicated chef upstairs because waiting for the lift burns food.

**Kevin 4** A rectangle around a **central fireplace that shoots fire everywhere**. Top: return, three
boards, serve. Left: a mixer and two steamers with a bin at the bottom. Right: the mirror, with the sink
and extinguisher instead of the bin. Bottom: all five crates with plates between them. One chef on fires
and dishes, the rest on the chain.

**Kevin 5** Two 2×5 work rectangles, one top one bottom, that **swap sides when wind blows** — and the
wind pushes chefs too. Cars drive between them. Bottom has two steamers and three boards plus flour;
top has two mixers and every other crate. Plates top-left, sink top-right, return + bin bottom-left,
plate + extinguisher bottom-right, serve middle-right. Two chefs, one per band, constantly re-anchoring.

**Kevin 6** Four quadrants each with one gap, ringed and cross-linked by conveyors that push you
clockwise or inward — so the belts make working *outside* a quadrant nearly impossible. Top-left:
steamers and sink. Top-right: boards and extinguisher. Bottom-left: four crates. Bottom-right: mixers,
return, bin, carrot. Serve is far left. Everything must be handed off through the belt ring.

**Kevin 7** Top and bottom halves with **a moving platform on each side** — the left platform carries
both mixers and the carrot box, the right carries both steamers and the fish box. Top: return, bin, beef
and flour, a plate, two boards. Bottom: sink, two boards, two plates, prawn. A single centre counter is
the only fixed link, so the two cooking stations are literally in transit.

**Kevin 8** A **fast conveyor floor** fed by five ingredient dispensers (beef, prawn, flour, carrot,
fish) that merges 5→1, then a rotating section splits it 3 ways into bins. Left side: steamers, sink,
extinguisher, board, two plates. Right side: mixers, serve, return, board, two plates. Hand-offs must be
made with the receiver *downstream* of the giver on the belt, never on the same tile.
