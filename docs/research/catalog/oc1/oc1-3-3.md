# oc1 3-3 — Ravenous Road
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-3_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/3-3.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00, no prep time. Unlock: the Stars to Unlock table says 26; the infobox says 16. The table is the better source. 1-4 players. Infobox 4-star difficulty Hard. **The first kitchen in the series to serve two recipe families at once** — the level page says so in its opening line.
- Six variants across two families: the three soups plus Chips, Fried Fish and Fish and Chips. That is the largest recipe list in worlds 1 to 3 and the reason `orders.max` is set higher here than anywhere else `(estimate)`: initial 2, every 20 s, max 5, 110 s each.
- OC1 star columns blank. 3-star only, from the overview table: 1P 150, 2P 270 — the biggest gap between the one- and two-player targets in the first three worlds, which reads as a level designed around two chefs specialising.
- Infobox `plates = No`: no sink, plates come back clean at the plate return on the supply truck. Four plates, two on each cooking truck, read off the screenshot; the wiki gives no count.

## Dynamics
- Three truck beds that drive closer together and further apart, as in 2-1. The wiki gives no period, so the timing is `(estimate)`: the same roughly 12 s cycle used for 2-1, with the supply truck's gap to each cooking truck opening and closing out of phase so that only one delivery window is open at a time.
- The Strategies section adds a genuinely useful detail: "pay attention to when the truck is about to move to avoid falling, as it swerves around a little bit in Overcooked: All You Can Eat before driving away". There is a tell.
- Falling off costs 5 seconds — the infobox states it.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki states the plan outright: "always have one chef on the Fish and Chips side and one on the Soup side to avoid burnt food", and "if you have three or more chefs, keep one on the supply truck".
- That is the design in one sentence: soup and fish burn on independent clocks, so a chef who tries to cover both will burn one of them. The two-player game is therefore one specialist per cooking truck, both reaching across to the supply truck for parts.
- The single-player tip shows the cost: "take items for only one Soup recipe to not overflow the chef with Ingredients. You can take multiple Fish and Potatoes to the Fish and Chips truck" — because a pot needs three of the same thing and a fryer needs one of anything.
- The bottleneck is the supply truck. Five crates, one serving counter and one plate return all live on it, so it is both the source and the sink; the cooking trucks are otherwise self-contained.

## Recreate checklist
- Throwing, and moving platforms — as 2-1, and here across three beds rather than two.
- Fish and Chips: recipes, deep fryers, frying baskets, fish and potato crates.
- Two recipe families live in one kitchen at once, which the order system has to draw from.
- Plates that return clean with no sink.
- A fall hazard with a 5 s time penalty.
