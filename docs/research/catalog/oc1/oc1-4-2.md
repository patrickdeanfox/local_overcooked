# oc1 4-2 — Munch Mansion (darkness)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-2_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/4-2.jpg` (dark, as played), `docs/research/screens/oc1/4-2-lit.jpg` (same kitchen with the lights on)

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **35 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 22.
  Player counts 1-4.
- Recipes: Onion Soup, Tomato Soup, Mushroom Soup — 3 order variants. Cadence not published:
  initial 2, one new order every 18 s, max 4 on screen, 60 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1**: 3★ only — 100 (1P), 110 (2P), from the
  [Overcooked! Levels](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Overcooked!_Levels) overview table.
  **AYCE**: 80/180/280/440 (1P), 160/340/540/860 (2P), 260/560/880/1360 (3P), 280/600/940/1460 (4P).
  **AYCE Switch**: 80/180/300/480 (1P), 140/320/520/800 (2P), 220/500/780/1200 (3P),
  200/440/700/1100 (4P). Note the OC1 2P number (110) is barely above the 1P one — the only
  level in the game where that is true.
- Plates: sink kitchen (`plates=Yes`). 3 clean plates, starting on the north counter run beside the
  serving counter, in the top-right corner. Dirty plates return at the plate return under the serve.

  vertical on screen, so the kitchen measures roughly 15 columns by 10 rows. The stations and their
  order along each run are certain (the lit capture and the wiki Overview agree on every one); only

## Dynamics
- **Darkness** — the only kitchen in Overcooked! that uses it. Nothing moves; the level is lit only
  by cones from lamps carried by the chefs, so the layout has to be memorised. The wiki gives no
  cone angle or radius: cone ~90° and ~3 tiles `(estimate)`.
- Raw ingredients **glow in the dark**, so dropped ingredients double as landmarks (wiki Strategies).
- No fire hazard beyond burnt pots, no falling, no moving furniture.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies: keep a picture of the lit kitchen to hand; failing that, mark places by leaving
  ingredients near them because they glow; and "split up to light up more area".
- Division of labour: the north corridor owns cooking and serving (two pots, sink, plates, serve),
  the south owns supply (tomato and mushroom crates). The onion crate is on the west wall, two thirds
  of the way down, so the onion loop is the longest walk on the level (23 tiles crate-to-serve).
- The bottleneck is navigation, not throughput. Two pots and three plates would be comfortable in a
  lit kitchen; the star thresholds (3★ at 100 for 1P) are the lowest of world 4 for that reason.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Darkness**: a per-chef light cone and an unlit render of everything outside it. Purely
   presentational — no sim change — but it is the whole level.
2. Glow-in-the-dark raw ingredients, so dropped items stay visible as landmarks.
3. Nothing else: 3 soups, 2 pots, sink, plates, bin, extinguisher and an island are all mechanics
   the clone already has. **4-2 is the most recreatable level in world 4.**
