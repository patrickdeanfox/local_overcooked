# oc1 6-4 — Cosmic Canteen (four rotating chambers)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/6-4_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/6-4.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **74 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 54.
  Player counts 1-4. The last numbered kitchen in the game.
- Recipes: **three families at once** — Salad, Tomato Salad, Onion Soup, Tomato Soup, Mushroom Soup,
  Burger, Lettuce Burger, Salad Burger. **8 order variants**, the most of any OC1 level, and the only
  numbered kitchen in Overcooked! that serves Salad. Cadence not published: initial 2, one new order
  every 20 s, max 4 on screen, 75 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 100/220/340/540 (1P), 120/280/460/720 (2P),
  140/320/520/820 (3P), 160/360/560/880 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 60/160/260/420 (1P),
  80/180/280/440 (2P), 160/360/580/920 (3P), 80/180/280/440 (4P). The JSON carries the AYCE-Switch
  numbers, **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: sink kitchen; the sink is in the centre. 4 plates, 2 in each of the pair-1 chambers.
  **OC1-only quirk:** "Dirty Plates will only appear at one Plate Return at a time, so do not wait at
  the other, empty one for dishes to come." The remaster changed this.

- Legend extensions used: `?` **rotation button** (left button turns the ring 90° counterclockwise,

- Station inventory: boards 2 (centre), **pot burners 2** and **pan burners 2** (centre, north wall),

  chamber), **plate returns 2**, bins 1 (centre), extinguishers 1 (centre, bottom-left), plates 4,
  rotation buttons 2, crates 6 — pair 1: lettuce (west) and mushroom (east); pair 2: tomato + onion
  (north) and bun + beef (south) — plain counters 26. Floor 44, counter 52, station 26.
- Spawns: the capture puts both chefs in the centre, at (6,4) and (8,5) `(estimate)`.
- Zones:
  - **Centre** (cols 5-10, rows 4-6): four burners and both buttons on the north wall; two chopping
    boards on the island; sink, bin and extinguisher on the south wall. Four plain counters. The wiki
    calls the space "very limited", and it is: 12 walkable tiles for the whole kitchen.
  - **Pair 1 chambers** (lettuce / mushroom): each has a serving counter, a plate return, two plates
    and one crate.
  - **Pair 2 chambers**: crates only — tomato + onion, and bun + beef.
  - Only the two chambers currently on opposite sides of the centre are reachable, through the `G`
    doorways. Rotating swaps which pair you have.

## Dynamics
- **Four chambers rotating on a ring.** Wiki: "The kitchen is centered around rotating the outside
  chambers to access different ingredients, both Plate Returns and both Service Counters. These can
  be rotated by pressing buttons inside the center chamber. Pressing the left button will rotate the
  structure 90 degrees counterclockwise, while pressing the right button rotates it 90 degrees
  clockwise. Only two chambers that are on opposite sides can be accessed at once."
- Rotation duration is not published — ~3 s per 90° step, with the doorways sealed for the duration
  `(estimate)`. Whether a chef can ride a chamber round is not stated `(estimate: yes, as in 5-1)`.
- No fall hazard, no conveyor, no timer-driven obstacle. The only clock is the rotation you choose.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies: "Do not stock up on Ingredients as you won't have a place to put them on and the
  middle chamber will become messy. Instead, handle only 2-3 orders at a time before going for new
  food." Plus the plate-return quirk above.
- The bottleneck is **counter space in the centre**, not cooking capacity. Four burners and two boards
  is generous, but with only four free counters and twelve floor tiles, anything you fetch and cannot
  immediately use blocks the kitchen.
- The intended division is one chef minding the centre (cooking, plating, washing) and one working
  the rotation, since serving and fetching both require having the right pair docked. With three
  recipe families in play, the rotation schedule *is* the plan.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Rotating chambers on a ring** — four blocks of tiles that cycle around the centre in 90° steps,
   carrying their stations and items, with only the opposite pair docked at once.
2. **Buttons** that trigger a discrete rotation in each direction (shared with 5-1 and 5-5).
3. **Salad recipe family** (chop and plate, no cooking) — the clone has no cook-free recipe.
4. **Three recipe families in one order pool**, with 8 variants.
5. **Two serving counters and two plate returns**, plus the OC1 rule that dirty plates only appear at
   one of them at a time.
6. Everything else is present: soups, burgers, pots, pans, boards, sink, bin, extinguisher.
