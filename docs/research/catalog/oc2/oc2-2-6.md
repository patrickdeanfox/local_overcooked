# oc2 2-6 — Moreish Mines
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/2-6_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 2-6.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00 (240 s). **Delayed timer** — the wiki Strategies section says in capitals that the clock does not start until the first order is sent, so build a stockpile first.
- Unlock: 14 stars. Playable 1–4 players. World 2 boss level.
- Recipes: Meat Burger and Cheese Burger, 2 order variants. First burger level.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~22 s, max 4, ~80 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Four plates, all starting on the rotating centre counter.
- Obstacles: moving tables, bottleneck, isolated chefs (infobox).
- Wiki discrepancy: the Overview puts the Service Counter and Plate Return "on the bottom left"; in the screenshot they are unmistakably on a ledge at the **bottom right**, past the circular platform. The screenshot is followed here.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 260/340/460/1180; 2P 400/540/740/1600; 3P 480/660/880/1900; 4P 480/660/880/2200. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **rotating-counter** — the seven-tile centre counter rotates counter-clockwise 90° about its middle tile (6,4) every 30 seconds, so the wall between the halves swings from vertical to horizontal and back. It carries the three pans and the four plates with it. Period 30 s.
- Hazard: fall into the pit at the bottom left
- Hazard: pan fire — the extinguisher is on the right half only

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki strategy: always keep one chef on each side or somebody is cut off from the serve window.
- Bottleneck: the rotation. Roles are dictated by which side of the counter you happen to be on when it turns, so the level is really about pre-positioning before each 30-second tick.
- Delayed timer means the correct opening is to pre-cook a wall of patties on the rotating counter before the first serve.
