# oc2 4-5 — Sushi City
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-5_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/4-5.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 38 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (4 order variants): Fish Sashimi, Cucumber Sushi, Fish Sushi, Fish & Cucumber Sushi.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 60 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 200/260/360 · 2P 280/360/480 · 3P 320/420/560 · 4P 320/420/560. The 4★ bonus tier is 650 / 950 / 950 / 950 — the 3- and 4-player charts are identical to each other. Scoring 400 or more here unlocks Kevin 6.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 2 plates. The wiki is explicit: two plates, one starting on each half. Plates cannot be thrown, so the belt-plus-button handshake is the only way one half can give the other a plate.

## Dynamics
- **conveyors** (period unknown) — a ring belt runs round the whole kitchen; two overlapping L belts in the middle end in bins, and a button on each side steers what is on them toward that side.
- **Hazards** — items ride into a bin if nobody presses the button.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's split: the bottom chef washes, puts cut ingredients on a clean plate and sends the plate up; the top chef keeps rice cooked, finishes the sushi, delivers, and sends dirty plates back down.
- The bottleneck is plate supply — two plates, no throwing, and the only transfer is a belt that will bin whatever is on it unless the receiving side presses its button.
- Because the button steers the belt toward the presser's own half, every transfer needs both chefs acting at the same moment.
