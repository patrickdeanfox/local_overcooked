# oc2 1-6 — Buffet Balloons → Sushi City
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-6_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 1-6.jpg, 1-6-shake1.jpg, 1-6-shake2.jpg, 1-6-sushicity.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 4:10 (250 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 6 stars. Playable 1–4 players. World 1 boss level; infobox `dynamic = Yes`.
- Recipes: phase 1 is Salad (Lettuce, Tomato, Cucumber & Tomato); phase 2 is Sushi (Fish, Cucumber). 5 order variants across the level. Salads already on the ticket list carry over into phase 2 but no new salad is added.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~20 s, max 5, ~80 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Three plates in phase 1 (one on the left counter, two on the bottom counter); phase 2 adds two more plates near the new serve window, so 5 `(estimate on the total)`.
- Obstacles: fire and conveyor belts (infobox).
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 200/240/340/1050; 2P 260/320/440/1500; 3P 300/380/520/2100; 4P 300/380/520/2000. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **phase-change** — at 2:20 elapsed (1:50 remaining) the balloon crashes into a sushi restaurant; the square becomes the centre of a much larger kitchen and the recipe list switches from salad to sushi.
- **fire** — the brazier in the middle of the balloon shoots a projectile to a random tile inside the balloon; it lands as a flame that blocks movement and dissipates on its own (the extinguisher speeds it up, but the extinguisher only exists after the crash). Rate not on the wiki.
- **sliders** — as the balloon starts to fall, the items on the left and right sides slide around the deck (the `1-6-shake1` and `1-6-shake2` screenshots).
- **conveyors** — phase 2 only: three ingredient conveyors feed fish, rice and nori into the ring.
- Hazard: floor fire: flame tiles block movement until they burn out

## Cooperation notes
- Phase 1 wiki split: one chef fetches vegetables and serves, the other chops and washes; with 3–4 players each chef owns one vegetable and the fourth washes.
- Phase 2 wiki split: one chef on rice and dishes, one on fish/cucumber and plating; with 3 chefs it is rice / fish / cucumber.
- Bottleneck: floor fire lands anywhere inside the balloon and can cut a chef off from the sink or the serve window; there is no extinguisher until the crash.

## Recreate checklist
- Mid-level layout change (a scripted phase swap at a fixed time) — the clone has nothing like it.
- Floor fire that spawns on random tiles and blocks movement.
- Ingredient conveyors (a belt that emits an ingredient) as well as ordinary belts.
- Recipe list that switches mid-level, with the old tickets carrying over.
- New ingredients: cucumber, nori, rice, fish.
