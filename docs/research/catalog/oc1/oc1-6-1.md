# oc1 6-1 — Savoury Seas (a chopping block that circles the ship)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/6-1_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/6-1.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **65 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 44.
  Player counts 1-4. 4-star difficulty "Hard".
- Recipes: Onion Soup, Tomato Soup, Mushroom Soup — 3 order variants. Cadence not published:
  initial 2, one new order every 20 s, max 4 on screen, 75 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 100/220/340/540 (1P), 260/560/880/1380 (2P),
  340/740/1160/1800 (3P), 320/700/1100/1700 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 80/180/280/460 (1P),
  220/480/740/1140 (2P), 200/460/720/1120 (3P), 200/440/700/1080 (4P). The JSON carries the
  AYCE-Switch numbers, **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: **no sink** (`plates=No`). 3 plates, stacked **on the plate return** on the north counter
  run beside the serving counter. Dirty plates come back clean.

## Layout
- Grid size: **16 x 10**, counted from `6-1.jpg`; confidence **low**. Method: the three ingredient
  boxes on the central run give a ~46 px tile and the north run reads as nine tiles at ~45 px, which
  puts the walkable deck at about 16 columns; rows come from the two deck bands either side of the
  central run. Confidence is low because the ship's deck has no walls — the play area's edges are
  barrels and cannons, so where the floor stops is a judgement call, and because the chopping block
  and two loose counters are somewhere on a circuit at any moment.

## Dynamics
- **A three-tile block circles the ship clockwise.** Wiki: "a set of two Chopping Boards and a
  Countertop between them that move around the ship in a rectangular pattern starting from the
  top-left corner and going clockwise. Once the structure slides upwards/downwards, a set of two
  lonely Countertops at the opposite side of the Chopping Boards shift as well, allowing for the
  chefs to change sides." Lap time, dwell at each corner and the size of the opening are not
  published — a lap in ~40 s with a ~4 s crossing window `(estimate)`.
- **Open cargo grates** at (10,2) and (3,7) are permanent holes in the deck: not walkable, and
  nothing can be put on them.
- No fall hazard (the grates are obstacles, not pits), no fire source other than burnt pots.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies, in order of importance: "Using the time to switch sides is absolutely necessary
  for a Singleplayer run, as chopping with one chef is simply too slow"; "Always keep the Pots at the
  top to have them as close to the Service Counter as you can"; and an alternate plan where both
  chefs live on the chopping-board side, carry pots from burner to burner, and serve whenever the
  serving counter comes within reach.
- The bottleneck is that **the only two chopping boards are on the moving block**. Chopping happens
  wherever the block currently is, so the whole kitchen re-forms around it every lap.
- Because there is no sink, plates are cheap and the pressure is entirely on chop-and-boil throughput.

## Recreate checklist
1. **A counter group that travels a closed rectangular circuit** carrying its stations (and items) —
   the clone's `sliders` do one axis only; this needs a rectangular waypoint path around the level.
2. **Coupled movement**: when the block slides, two other counters elsewhere shift to open a gap.
   That is a scripted pair, not two independent dynamics.
3. **Empty burners** and **loose pots** — cookware that starts on a counter and must be carried to a
   burner. The clone's `S` starts with a pot welded to the burner.
4. **Hole tiles** (the cargo grates) that are neither floor nor counter.
5. Everything else is present: soups, boards, bin, extinguisher, serving counter, plate return.
