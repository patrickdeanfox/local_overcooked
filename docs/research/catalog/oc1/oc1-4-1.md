# oc1 4-1 — Munch Mansion

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-1_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/4-1.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately (infobox `preptime=No`). Unlock: **29 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the level infobox says
  20 stars, and `docs/research/oc1-levels.md` treats the Stars-to-Unlock table as the better source.
  Player counts 1-4 (the Strategies section explicitly addresses a 4-player split).
- Recipes: Pizza, Pepperoni Pizza, Mushroom Pizza — 3 order variants. The wiki gives no order
  cadence: initial 2, one new order every 20 s, at most 4 on screen, 60 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1**: 3★ only — 120 (1P), 200 (2P), from the
  [Overcooked! Levels](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Overcooked!_Levels) overview table; the
  level page's OC1 columns are blank. **AYCE**: 100/240/380/600 (1P), 160/360/580/900 (2P),
  260/580/920/1440 (3P), 300/640/1000/1560 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 100/220/340/540 (1P),
  160/340/540/840 (2P), 180/420/660/1040 (3P), 220/480/740/1160 (4P).
- Plates: sink kitchen (`plates=Yes`, and the Overview says the moving counters switch which chef
  can do the dishes). 3 clean plates, and they start **on the moving counters**, not on a fixed
  counter — in this capture they sit on the run north of the right room. Dirty plates come back at
  the plate return on the right wall.

## Layout
- Grid size: **16 x 12**, counted from `4-1.jpg`; confidence **low**. Method: the top counter run
  reads `counter, extinguisher, counter, oven, counter, oven, counter, oven, counter, bin, counter`
  = 11 tiles at ~41.5 px pitch; the bottom run reads 3 counters + 5 crates + 2 counters = 10 tiles
  at ~51 px pitch; solving those two pitches for the vanishing point puts both runs on the same
  column span and gives ~16 columns and ~12 rows for the cross-shaped floor plan. Confidence is low
  because **the interior counters float**, so any grid is one frame of a level that re-partitions
  itself every few seconds.

- Legend extensions used: `N` oven (3 of them on the north wall), `H` cheese crate,
  `&` dough crate, `!` pepperoni crate. Everything else is the standard legend.
- Station inventory: boards 2, pot burners 0, pan burners 0, fryers 0, **ovens 3**, mixers 0,
  steamers 0, sinks 1 (`W`+`D`), serve counters 1 (2 tiles tall), plate returns 1, bins 1,
  extinguishers 1, plates 3 (`p`), crates 5 (dough, tomato, cheese, mushroom, pepperoni),
  plain counters 39. Floor 109, counter 59, station 20.
- Spawns: both chefs stand in the checkered rooms in the capture — chef 1 at (4,5) in the left room,
  chef 2 at (11,5) in the right room `(estimate)`.
- Zones:
  - **North corridor** (rows 1-2): the three ovens, the bin, the extinguisher. Reaches the left
    room through the gap at row 3, cols 3-7.
  - **Left room** (cols 1-7, rows 4-7): the sink on the west wall, the two chopping boards on its
    south counter run. Walk-connected to the right room along row 7 in this frame.
  - **Right room** (cols 9-14, rows 4-7): the serving counter and plate return on the east wall,
    the three plates on its north counter run.
  - **South corridor** (rows 9-10): all five ingredient crates on the south wall. Reached from the
    right room down cols 9-11.
  - Items cross zones by **walking** when the floating counters leave a gap, and by placing on the
    shared counter runs otherwise. No throwing is required.

## Dynamics
- **Free-floating counters.** The wiki: "Countertops that do not move back and forth in a single
  direction, but rather float around freely… These Countertops move every once in a while, blocking
  off the chefs and switching from them having to do dishes and serving." Two of them carry chopping
  stations, three carry plates. No period, path or phase is published — period `(estimate)` ~20 s
  between repositions, path unknown.
- No other hazard: no fire source beyond burnt food, no falling, no pedestrians.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies: "Split up between top and bottom (and left and right if you have 4 players!)".
- The intended division is oven-side vs crate-side: one chef feeds dough/cheese/tomato from the
  south crates, the other bakes at the three north ovens. The bottleneck is that the moving counters
  decide, minute by minute, which chef owns the sink and which owns the serving counter, so both
  chefs must be able to do both jobs.
- Three ovens against one plate-washing sink and three plates makes plates, not ovens, the limit.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Oven station** and the pizza recipe family (assemble on flattened dough, then bake) — the clone
   has no oven and no multi-ingredient assembly onto a base item.
2. **Cheese, dough and pepperoni crates and their chop products** (shred, flatten, slice).
3. **Free-floating counters**: counters that translate along a path and re-partition the floor. The
   clone has `sliders` (one axis, fixed amplitude) which is a usable approximation; a faithful version
   needs a waypoint path and counters that carry their items with them.
4. Plates that start on a moving counter rather than on a fixed tile.
