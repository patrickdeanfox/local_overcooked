# oc2 5-4 — Conjurer's Kitchen
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-4_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/5-4.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 54 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (2 order variants): Pancake, Chocolate Pancake.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 160/200/280 · 2P 260/340/460 · 3P 240/300/400 · 4P 240/300/400. The 4★ bonus tier is 1150 / 1450 / 1450 / 1700 — very high relative to the 3★ bar, which is the pattern for the first level of a new recipe family.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 2 plates. The wiki is explicit: the two plates start in the top-left section with the sink and the plate return, which is the section furthest from the mixers.

## Dynamics
- **stairs-portal-swap** (35 s) — both staircases disappear and are replaced by portals roughly every 35 s, then come back.
- **moving-portal** (period unknown) — a third portal drifts left and right over the two top sections and decides where the bottom portal delivers you — sometimes into the pit.
- **Hazards** — the pit under the top sections; the moving portal can drop you into it.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's 2-player split: one chef makes pancakes in the bottom section, the other washes, throws chocolate right when needed, plates and serves, and watches the moving portal.
- The bottleneck is vertical movement. Every ~35 s the stairs vanish and the only route down is a portal, and coming back up puts you wherever the drifting portal happens to be — sometimes in the pit.
- The wiki's shortcuts: chocolate can be thrown from the top-left straight to the top-right section, and flour can be thrown down — carefully, since the bin and the right mixer are both in the landing zone.
