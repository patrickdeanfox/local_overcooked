# oc2 4-1 — Sushi City
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-1_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/4-1.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 3:00 (180 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 28 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (5 order variants): Fish Sashimi, Prawn Sashimi, Cucumber Sushi, Fish Sushi, Fish & Cucumber Sushi.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 15 s, at most 5 on screen, 60 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 140/180/240 · 2P 260/340/460 · 3P 380/480/640 · 4P 380/480/640. The 4★ bonus tier is 620 / 1000 / 1100 / 1340.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 2 plates. Both plates start on the bottom counter of the left kitchen, next to the cucumber crate; the plate return is on the right, so every plate crosses the road at least once per dish.

## Dynamics
- **cars** (period unknown) — cars drive down the road and run chefs over while the traffic light is green.
- **dispenser-conveyors** (period unknown) — two belts spawn ingredients instead of crates: rice + nori on the left, prawn + fish on the right.
- **Hazards** — cars: run over on the road while the light is green.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's own split: the left chef cooks extra rice and cuts extra seafood; the right chef throws seafood left, washes plates and plates up.
- The bottleneck is the road. The wiki says to avoid the centre of the map entirely and dash across when you must, which turns the crossing into a scheduled event rather than a walk.
- The wiki's prep tip is to put nori onto rice-loaded plates in advance so the plate is one chop away from finished when the seafood lands.
