# oc2 4-2 — Ravenous Rapids
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-2_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/4-2.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 30 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (5 order variants): Lettuce Salad, Tomato Salad, Chips, Nuggets, Nuggets & Chips.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 240/300/400 · 2P 240/300/420 · 3P 280/360/480 · 4P 280/360/480. The 4★ bonus tier is 790 / 1080 / 900 / 1150.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 4 plates. Two plates sit on the top raft's top counter row between the sink and the two fryers; the other two flank the boards on the bottom raft's bottom row. The plate return is on the bottom raft, beside the serve counter.

- **Legend extensions used**

  - `%` — potato crate

  - `_` — river — falling in costs a respawn
  - `> < ^ v` — floor-level conveyor belt; each raft has one L-shaped belt that ends in that raft's bin, and the two Ls meet when the rafts line up
- **Station inventory** (counted from the grid above): 3 boards; 2 deep fryers; 1 sink; 1 serve counter; 1 plate return; 2 bins; 1 extinguisher; 4 plates; 12 plain counters; crates: chicken, lettuce, potato, tomato.
  None of: burners with pots, burners with pans, ovens, mixers, steamers.
- **Spawns** (best guess, the wiki never states them): (5,2), (5,9).
- **Zones**
  - **top raft** — fryer x2, sink, board, tomato crate, lettuce crate, trash, plate x2. → bottom raft via throw; → bottom raft via conveyor (plates only)
  - **bottom raft** — board x2, serve, plateReturn, extinguisher, potato crate, chicken crate, trash, plate x2. → top raft via throw; → top raft via conveyor (plates only)
- The two L-belts are the only channel for plates, which cannot be thrown. Note that on Nintendo Switch players 1 and 3 are the ones with the serve counter and players 2 and 4 are on the other raft.

## Dynamics
- **moving-platforms** (period unknown) — the two rafts alternate between being connected vertically and horizontally; the belts only line up while they are close.
- **Hazards** — river: fall in and respawn.

## Cooperation notes
- Roles are set by geography: the top raft can chop, deep fry and wash but cannot serve; the bottom raft can chop and serve but has no fryer.
- Ingredients cross by throwing; plates cross only by being left on a belt while the rafts line up, so the two logistics problems use different channels and have to be scheduled separately.
- The wiki's advice is to keep lettuce and tomato pre-cut, call out what you need, and always send dirty plates over while the rafts are connected.
- Endgame trick from the wiki: a plate-dash — dash forward while holding a plate and hit throw — moves a plate a short distance across.
