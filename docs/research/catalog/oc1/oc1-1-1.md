# oc1 1-1 — Treacle Town
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-1_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/1-1.jpg, oc1/1-1-ayce.jpg

## Facts
- Infobox time is "2:30 until 1 order complete" — 150 s that does not start until the first dish is served, which the Strategies section repeats ("you can stock up on chopped Onions before it starts"). Unlock: 1 star per both the infobox and the Stars to Unlock table; the clone ships it as 0 because it skips Intro Apocalypse, which hands that star out free. 1-4 players.
- Onion Soup only — one recipe, one variant. No order cadence anywhere on the wiki. The clone runs initial 2, one new ticket every 18 s, at most 4 live, 60 s each, measured in `docs/LEVELS.md`; that is a tuning result, not a wiki fact.
- OC1 Star Chart, filled in: 1P 10 / 40 / 60, 2P 10 / 40 / 60, 3P 20 / 40 / 100, 4P 20 / 40 / 120. Provenance `oc1`. The Overcooked! Levels overview table disagrees on the 2P 3-star (80); the level page's own chart is preferred.
- Infobox `plates = Yes`: dirty plates return to the plate return at (0,4) and are washed at the sink on the east wall, (12,2) basin over (12,3) rack. Two clean plates start on the south counter at (9,7) and (10,7).

## Dynamics
- Nothing moves. The infobox lists `dynamic = No` and the only obstacle is "Countertop that needs to be walked around".

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies, all three of them, describe the intended split: pass onions over the island so one chef only chops; pour finished soups onto plates immediately, because one pot is the whole cooking capacity; and pre-chop during the stopped clock.
- The bottleneck is the single pot, and behind it the two plates — a soup cannot be poured until a clean plate exists, so the sink is inside the loop rather than beside it.
- The island detour is what makes the hand-off worth learning: walking round it costs about 20 tiles, passing over it costs none.
