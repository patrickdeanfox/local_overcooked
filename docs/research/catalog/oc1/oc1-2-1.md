# oc1 2-1 — Ravenous Roads
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/2-1_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/2-1.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00, no prep time. Unlock: the Stars to Unlock table says 10; the infobox says 7. The table is the better source. 1-4 players. The level that introduces the death penalty — the infobox spells it out as "Moving Trucks, 5 second penalty for falling off".
- The three burgers, three variants. No cadence on the wiki. `orders` here is `(estimate)`, shaped on 1-6 (same recipes, same crossing tax) and slowed slightly because the gap between the trucks is a harder wall than 1-6's seam: initial 2, every 26 s, max 4, 110 s each.
- The level page's OC1 star columns are blank. Only the 3-star exists, from the Overcooked! Levels overview table: 1P 180, 2P 240. Provenance `oc1`; the 1-star and 2-star are left `null` rather than guessed. The AYCE and AYCE-Switch columns on the page are much higher and are not OC1 numbers.
- Infobox `plates = No` and the Overview confirms "There is no Sink in this kitchen", so dirty plates never appear: served plates come back clean at the plate return on the upper truck, (8,2). Three clean plates start on the upper truck's north counter at (5,0), (6,0), (7,0).

## Layout
- Grid size: 13 x 8, counted from oc1/2-1.jpg; method: seam detection along each truck's counter run — 47 px per tile on the upper truck at screen y 195, 53.5 px on the lower truck at y 545 — then both spans converted to tile counts about a vanishing x of 620, which puts each truck bed at exactly 9 columns and the lower truck 4 columns east of the upper one; confidence **med**

- **Lower truck** (columns 4-12, rows 4-7): beef, lettuce and tomato crates down the west wall at (4,4), (4,5), (4,6); the trash bin at (12,4); one chopping board at (12,6) and another at (9,7); the fire extinguisher at (5,7).
- **The highway** (row 3): open road. Falling costs 5 s.
- Items cross by being thrown, or walked when the trucks are close. Nothing can be handed over a counter — the two beds never share an edge.

## Dynamics
- The two trucks drive "closer and further from each other, allowing to pass between them if they are driving like said formerly". No period, no amplitude and no dwell time on the wiki, so every number is `(estimate)`.
- A workable model: the beds' lateral gap closes to zero and opens to about two tiles on a cycle of roughly 12 s `(estimate)`, with the closed window lasting a third of it. While it is open the only route is a throw.
- The 3-3 page adds a detail worth copying: in All You Can Eat the truck "swerves around a little bit before driving away", i.e. there is a tell before the gap opens.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's one strategy line is the whole level: "much like in 1-6, prep up Ingredients beforehand and throw them all to the upper truck as soon as they are close". Lower truck preps, upper truck cooks and serves.
- That makes the lower chef's job batching. Chopping three ingredients and throwing them one at a time wastes the window; the play is to pile chopped parts on the lower truck's counters and empty the pile in one pass.
- The bottleneck is the crossing window, not the three pans and not the three plates. With no sink the plates never leave circulation, so the only way to stall is to have parts on the wrong truck.

## Recreate checklist
- Throwing. Without it the level cannot be played at all — there is no walkable route between the two beds for most of the cycle.
- Moving platforms: two rigid bodies of tiles that translate relative to each other, with stations and items riding along.
- A fall hazard with a time penalty, and a respawn.
- Plates that come back clean with no sink (`plates.mode` needs a third mode, or map onto `stack` at the plate return's tile).
