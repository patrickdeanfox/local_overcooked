# -- Settings: difficulty, seed mode, free play, and that they survive a reload -------------
#
# Walks the title's settings rows, then starts 1-4 twice on a pinned seed and once more on the
# daily seed, reloading the page in between so the saved settings have to be read back.
# Rows, in cursor order: six levels, Players, Difficulty, Seed, Seed number, Free play,
# Controllers - so Difficulty is index 7, Seed 8, Seed number 9 and Free play 10.
