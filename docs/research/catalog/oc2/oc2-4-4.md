# oc2 4-4 — Moreish Mines
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-4_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/4-4.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 3:30 (210 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 35 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (3 order variants): Chips, Nuggets, Nuggets & Chips.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 220/280/380 · 2P 300/380/520 · 3P 300/380/520 · 4P 300/380/520. The 4★ bonus tier is 800 / 980 / 1100 / 1180.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 3 plates. Plate count is not on the wiki; three is an estimate from the round white objects on the two bottom counter rows. The plate return and the serve counter are both on the right.

## Dynamics
- **wind** (13 s) — gusts along the top and bottom bridges flip direction every ~13 s and always oppose each other, so one bridge is always a headwind.
- **sliders** (13 s) — short counters on the far left and right walls move up and down at roughly the wind tempo.
- **Hazards** — acid pit between the halves.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's 2-player split is one chef on potatoes, one on chicken; with 3 the extra chef washes and chops; with 4 the two fryer minders also wash (two chefs can wash the same sink at once, which is much faster).
- The bottleneck is that only the right side can serve and only the left side has the sink, so both bridges are permanently busy — and one of them is always a headwind.
- Neither chef can complete a Nuggets & Chips order alone: chicken is on the left and potato on the right.
