# oc1 1-2 — Treacle Town
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-2_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/1-2.jpg, oc1/1-2-ayce.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00 flat, `preptime = No`, so the clock runs from the start. Unlock: 2 stars (infobox and the Stars to Unlock table agree). 1-4 players.
- Onion Soup and Tomato Soup — the level that introduces the second soup. Two variants. No cadence on the wiki; the clone runs initial 2, every 26 s, max 4, 90 s each after a playtest showed the first guess (every 18 s) produced more tickets than any pair could serve.
- OC1 Star Chart, filled in: 1P 20 / 60 / 80, 2P 20 / 60 / 150, 3P 20 / 60 / 200, 4P 20 / 60 / 210. Provenance `oc1`.
- `plates = Yes`: plate return at (15,3), sink basin (0,3) with its rack at (0,2). Two clean plates start one on each side of the crossing, at (5,2) and (9,2).

## Dynamics
- Pedestrians walk the crossing from both sides and knock chefs about. The wiki gives no interval, speed or count, so every number here is `(estimate)`.
- The clone models it as two `pedestrians` dynamics so arrivals stagger: the outer lanes (x 6 and x 8) walk down every 7 s starting at 1.0 s, the middle lane (x 7) walks up every 7 s starting at 4.5 s, both at 2.5 tiles/s. That leaves two windows of about 1.5 s per cycle, enough for a chef at 4.2 tiles/s to clear three columns.

## Cooperation notes
- One chef per half is the obvious reading, but everything a soup needs — crate, board, pot, plate, hatch — is split across the road, so every dish crosses at least twice unless the chefs hand items over the counter columns beside the crossing.
- The real bottleneck the clone's playtest found is geometry rather than traffic: column 9 is counter at every row except y=1 and column 5 opens only at rows 3-4, so every ingredient funnels through the single tile (9,1). Two chefs cannot pass in it.
- The wiki's own tip is to route around the bottom rather than fight the crossing, which turns the pedestrians from a wall into a tax.
