# oc2 6-4 — Buffet Balloons
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/6-4_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/6-4.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 79 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (6 order variants): Meat Burger, Lettuce & Cheese Burger, Tomato & Lettuce Burger, Pizza, Chicken Pizza, Pepperoni Pizza.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 140/180/260 · 2P 260/320/440 · 3P 340/420/580 · 4P 340/420/580. The 4★ bonus tier is 600 / 900 / 950 / 1150. Earning a x5 tip combo here unlocks the last hidden level, Kevin 8.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 2 plates. Plate count is not on the wiki; two is read from the round discs on the far-left and far-right counters. The sink is on the right, the plate return is beside the serve counter at the top of the deck.

## Dynamics
- **driven-crate-islands** (period unknown) — two control sticks drive two 2x2 ingredient-box islands around the deck; only the two boxes on the far side of an island face the kitchen, so the islands have to be flipped top-to-bottom to reach the other two.
- **Hazards** — none.

## Cooperation notes
- The task is purely communicative: read the ticket list and pre-position the islands, because only the two boxes on the far side of an island face the kitchen. The wiki says the islands mostly alternate between parked at the top (to use their bottom two boxes) and parked at the bottom (to use their top two).
- The wiki's 2-player split: the left chef keeps a steady stream of burger and pizza bases going; the right chef chops pizza toppings, adds burger garnish and keeps clean plates to hand.
- Burgers and pizzas are ordered together and share cheese, so a badly parked island stalls both recipe families at once.
