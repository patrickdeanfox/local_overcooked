# oc2 4-6 — Moreish Mines
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-6_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/4-6.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 3:30 (210 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 41 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (3 order variants): Beef Burrito, Chicken Burrito, Mushroom Burrito.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 180/240/320 · 2P 380/420/580 · 3P 500/640/860 · 4P 500/640/860. The 4★ bonus tier is 800 / 1050 / 1250 / 1450. Note the 2-player 1★ (380) is more than double the 1-player 1★ (180) — the steepest jump in world 4.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 2 plates. Plate count is not on the wiki; two is an estimate from the round white objects on the bottom-left counter and the middle island. The plate return is bottom-right, beside the serve counter.

## Dynamics
- **swapping-counters** (30 s) — the counter sets on the leftmost and rightmost walls alternate what they hold every 30 seconds; the left set starts with rice, chicken and mushroom, the right set with two boards plus tortilla and beef.
- **conveyors** (period unknown) — the top and bottom links are walkable belts, right and left respectively.
- **fire** (period unknown) — the infobox lists fire as an obstacle.
- **Hazards** — mine shaft in the middle; fire.

## Cooperation notes
- Unusually for OC2 the wiki's recommended 2-player play is deliberately *low*-coordination: move one pot and one pan to each side at the start, then have each chef own whole orders end to end, wash their own plates, and mind their own stoves. Food is not thrown across unless the thrower is idle.
- The only forced contact is the 30-second crate rotation: the crate you need may be on the other side's wall when the counters swap.
- A third player becomes a dedicated server and dishwasher so the other two never leave prep.
