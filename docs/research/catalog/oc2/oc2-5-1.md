# oc2 5-1 — Sushi City
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-1_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/5-1.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 44 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (4 order variants): Meat Burger, Cucumber Sushi, Fish Sushi, Fish & Cucumber Sushi.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 240/300/420 · 2P 280/360/480 · 3P 300/380/520 · 4P 300/380/520. The 4★ bonus tier is 700 / 1000 / 1250 / 1400.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 3 plates. The wiki is explicit: all three plates start on the middle platform's counter column, so the ferry driver controls plate supply for both banks. The sink and the plate return are both on the right bank.

## Dynamics
- **steerable-platform** (period unknown) — a chef standing at the joystick drives the middle platform left and right; it is the only walkable link between the banks and it carries the clean plates.
- **Hazards** — river: fall in and respawn.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's split: the left chef drives the platform; the right chef keeps clean plates coming and chops the ingredients that finish a dish.
- The bottleneck is the ferry, because it is both the only walkable link and where the clean plates live — the driver sets everyone's tempo.
- The wiki asks the chefs to agree when to leave clean plates in the middle so the left chef can load nori and rice onto them for sushi.
- Burgers and sushi are ordered together, and the beef crate is on the right while the pans are on the left, so no dish is one chef's job.
