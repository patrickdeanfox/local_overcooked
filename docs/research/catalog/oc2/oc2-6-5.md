# oc2 6-5 — Gourmet Galaxy
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/6-5_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/6-5.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 86 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (3 order variants): Meat Burger, Cheese Burger, Lettuce & Cheese Burger.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 180/220/300 · 2P 240/300/400 · 3P 240/300/420 · 4P 240/300/420. The 4★ bonus tier is 480 / 750 / 750 / 940.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 3 plates. The wiki is explicit: the three plates start in the bottom half with the sink and the extinguisher. The plate return is in the top half, so plates make a full lap through the middle belt every dish.

## Dynamics
- **sinking-sections** (90 s) — each half's left and right sections sink alternately every 45 s, telegraphed by a rumble; items left on a sinking section survive, pans keep burning, and fires go out underwater.
- **conveyors** (period unknown) — a circular belt in the middle is the only route between the halves.
- **Hazards** — a section sinking under you.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki's split: the bottom half cooks patties and washes; the top half assembles and serves, because the bottom half already carries the dishwashing load.
- Transfers go on a plate or in a bun through the middle belt ring — the wiki says to avoid sending whole pans, which just adds clutter and actions.
- The 45-second sink cycle sets the rhythm: start patties the moment a section rises, and take them off the heat just before it goes down. Items on a sinking section survive; pans keep burning while submerged but the fires put themselves out.
