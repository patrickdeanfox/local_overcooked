# oc2 2-1 — Ravenous Rapids
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/2-1_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 2-1.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 2:20 (140 s), the shortest level in worlds 1–3. No delayed-timer note on the wiki.
- Unlock: 7 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Fast Food — Nuggets and Nuggets & Chips, 2 order variants. First deep-fryer level.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~20 s, max 4, ~70 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: no sink. Four plates on the bottom raft, two on each side rail.
- Obstacle: moving platforms (infobox). The two rafts drift.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 120/160/220/520; 2P 160/240/320/820; 3P 180/240/340/1000; 4P 180/240/340/1500. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

- Grid size: 12 x 10, counted from 2-1.jpg; method: the bottom raft's station run reads exactly as the wiki lists it — fryer, chicken, serve, plate return, potato, fryer — and those six single tiles set the tile width; the raft log planking is not tile-aligned so the depth is estimated; confidence med

- Legend extensions used:
  - `Y` deep fryer
  - `C` chicken crate
  - `%` potato crate
  - `_` river — a chef who walks in falls and respawns
- Station inventory: board 2, fryer 2, serve 1, plateReturn 1, trash 1, extinguisher 1, plate 4, counter 36; crates: chicken x1, potato x1
- Spawns (best guess): (5,2), (6,7)
- Zones:
  - **top raft** — 2 chopping boards. → bottom raft via throw; → bottom raft via walk-when-close
  - **bottom raft** — 2 deep fryers, chicken crate, potato crate, serve, plate return, trash, extinguisher, 4 plates. → top raft via throw or walk-when-close
- The purest supplier/cook split in the game: the top raft has nothing but two boards, so its only output is chopped food thrown down; the bottom raft has every other station.
- The rafts must come close enough to step across at least sometimes — the level has a 1-player Star Chart and a solo chef has to reach the boards `(estimate)`.

## Dynamics
- **moving-platforms** — both rafts drift on the river, opening and closing the gap between them. The wiki gives no period or amplitude.
- Hazard: fall in the river: chef respawns (delay not on the wiki)
- Hazard: fryer fire if a basket is left down too long

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki roles: top chef chops everything and throws it straight back down; bottom chef throws up two more of each than needed so the chopper is never idle, and does everything else.
- Bottleneck: two fryers against a 140 s clock — the fryers are the throughput limit, not the boards.
- With one player the raft crossing is the whole level; with two the throw arc is.

## Recreate checklist
- Deep fryer + frying basket station (chop → deep fry).
- Throwing items between separated zones.
- Moving platforms (drifting rafts) with a fall hazard beneath.
- New ingredients: chicken, potato. New recipe family: Fast Food.
- Clean plate return without a sink.
