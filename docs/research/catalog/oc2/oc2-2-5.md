# oc2 2-5 — Moreish Mines
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/2-5_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 2-5.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 3:30 (210 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 12 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Beef Burrito and Chicken Burrito, 2 order variants.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~26 s, max 4, ~100 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Four plates — two start bottom left, two top right.
- Obstacles: moving tables and bottleneck (infobox).
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 220/300/400/800; 2P 240/340/460/1000; 3P 320/440/600/1400; 4P 320/440/600/1400. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **sliders** — the four-crate counter slides left and right along the middle rail, so which ingredient a chef can reach changes constantly. Period not on the wiki.
- Hazard: fall into the chasm: chef respawns (delay not on the wiki)
- Hazard: pan fire

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki strategy is one line and it is the whole level: because the ingredients move, over-chop while a crate is in reach and throw the surplus over to the boards.
- Bottleneck: the rail. Every plate has to cross it because the pots are left and the serve is right.
- The chef who happens to be beside the crates when they arrive becomes the supplier for the next 30 seconds, so roles swap on the crates' timer rather than by agreement.
