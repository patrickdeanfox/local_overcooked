# oc1 3-4 — Glazed Glacier
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-4_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/3-4.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00, no prep time. Unlock: the Stars to Unlock table says 29; the infobox says 18. The table is the better source. 1-4 players. The last level of world 3.
- Chips, Fried Fish and Fish and Chips again, three variants. No cadence on the wiki; `orders` is `(estimate)`: initial 2, every 24 s, max 4, 110 s each — slower than 3-1 because every dish crosses the river.
- OC1 star columns blank. 3-star only, from the overview table: 1P 170, 2P 180 — nearly the same for one and two players, which is unusual and suggests the crossing, not the cooking, caps the score.
- `plates = Yes` and the wiki gives the count: **two Plates**, with the Strategies section warning to "wash plates as soon as you see them pop in to the Plate Return due to there being only two". Sink at the bottom of the west bank, plate return on the east bank.

## Layout
- Grid size: 15 x 8, counted from oc1/3-4.jpg; method: each bank's counter runs counted separately — the west bank's north run reads bin, counter, two fryers, counters; its west wall reads fish crate, potato crate, extinguisher; the east bank's north run reads counters, two plates, one fryer. The river's width comes from the gap between the two banks' inner counter edges; confidence **med**

- **East bank** (columns 9-13, rows 1-6): two clean plates at (10,0) and (11,0); the third, "easy-to-access" deep fryer at (12,0); the two-tile serving hatch at (14,3)-(14,4); the plate return at (14,5); both chopping boards at (10,7) and (12,7).
- **The river** (columns 6-8): open water, five seconds if you fall in. Ice floes drift down it and are the only way across.
- Nothing can be handed over a counter - the banks are three columns apart. Everything rides a floe. The two `=` rows are one frame of the drift: at that instant a floe bridges the river and the banks are connected, which is the only time a walking route exists at all.

## Dynamics
- **Ice floes as moving platforms.** "The chefs must use those floes to cross from the left side to the right side, and vice-versa." Sizes vary and the wiki gives no interval, speed or size distribution, so all of that is `(estimate)`: a floe every 6-8 s, drifting at about 1 tile/s, one to three tiles across.
- **Two floor materials in one kitchen.** "The snowy areas on the kitchen are not slippery, so keep that in mind, as switching from slippery to non-slippery movement can catch players off-guard." So the friction changes mid-level, which nothing else in the game does.
- **The river.** Falling in costs 5 seconds — the infobox calls it "Time Penalty +5 seconds for the chef that fell in".
- The Strategies section says to dash before stepping onto a floe "to clear the river in as few seconds as possible", which implies a floe is not a reliable ferry so much as a stepping stone.

## Cooperation notes
- The station split is total: every ingredient is on the west bank, every plate and both boards and the serving counter are on the east. A dish therefore crosses the river at least twice — raw ingredients east to be chopped, then either back west to fry or into the third fryer the wiki calls "easy-to-access" on the east side.
- That third fryer is the level's release valve, and the wiki says why: "fry the Chips/Fish in the rightmost Deep Fryer to avoid losing your chopped Ingredients when you cross the river". Losing a chopped ingredient to the water is the failure mode the level is built around.
- The bottleneck is the two plates. The sink is on the west bank, the plate return on the east, so washing means a crossing too, and the Strategies section tells you to do it the moment a plate appears rather than batching.
- So the natural two-player split is one chef per bank with the floes as a conveyor between them, and the level is really about timing hand-offs to the drift.

## Recreate checklist
- Moving platforms a chef can stand on and ride, with items riding too.
- A fall hazard with a 5 s time penalty and a respawn.
- Slippery floor, and a second floor material on the same map that is not slippery.
- Fish and Chips: recipes, deep fryers, frying baskets, fish and potato crates.
- A dash, since the wiki's crossing advice depends on it.
