- **Recipes** (23 order variants): Tomato Salad, Cucumber & Tomato Salad, Prawn Sashimi, Cucumber Sushi, Fish Sashimi, Fish Sushi, Nuggets, Tomato Pasta, Nuggets & Chips, Fish & Prawn Pasta, Cheese Burger, Lettuce & Cheese Burger, Tomato & Lettuce Burger, Beef Burrito, Mushroom Burrito, Steamed Beef, Steamed Carrot, Pizza, Pepperoni Pizza, Pancake, Chocolate Cake, Chocolate Pancake, Carrot Cake.

- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 500/1000/1500 · 2P 500/1000/1500 · 3P 500/1000/1500 · 4P 500/1000/1500. ## Dynamics
- **phases** (period unknown) — progress-driven, not clock-driven: the layout changes once the burgers have been delivered, into a portal-and-conveyor kitchen with mixers and ovens; the ground breaks after the last burger.
- **gate** (period unknown) — the serve counter and plate return start behind a gate; a button on either side opens it.
- **portals** (period unknown) — phase 2 only; items can be thrown through them.
- **conveyors** (period unknown) — phase 2 only.
- **sliders** (period unknown) — moving tables are listed in the obstacles field.
- **Hazards** — the ground breaking after the last burger.

## Cooperation notes
- The order list is fixed, not random: two of every dish in the game, roughly in the order the dishes were introduced, always the same sequence. That makes the level a memorisation exercise rather than a reactive one.
- The wiki's only strategy notes are to work one dish at a time in multiplayer, to assist with the other player's food and the door button, and that items can be thrown through the portals.
- The first bottleneck is the gate: the serve counter and plate return start closed and a button on either side opens them, so the first delivery needs two chefs or one chef doing two trips.
- The layout change is progress-driven, not clock-driven — it fires once the burgers are delivered — so the team controls when it happens.
