# oc1 5-4 — Melting Magma (figure-8 orbit)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-4_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/5-4.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **54 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 35.
  Player counts 1-4. 4-star difficulty is rated **"Insane"** — the only level in Overcooked! 1 with
  that rating, and the hardest 4-star in the game.
- Recipes: Beef Burrito, Chicken Burrito — 2 order variants. Cadence not published: initial 2, one
  new order every 24 s, max 4 on screen, 90 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 120/260/420/660 (1P), 140/320/500/780 (2P),
  160/360/560/880 (3P), 180/400/620/980 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 140/300/480/760 (1P),
  100/240/380/600 (2P), 140/320/520/820 (3P), 160/340/540/840 (4P). The JSON carries the
  AYCE-Switch numbers, **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: **no sink** (`plates=No`). 2 clean plates, sitting **on top of the plate return** beside
  the serving counter on the far left. Dirty plates come back clean.

## Layout
- Grid size: **17 x 9**, counted from `5-4.jpg`; confidence **low**. Method: the counter blocks in
  the middle of each platform give ~55 px tiles, sizing each rock platform at about 6 x 5; the gap
  between them is ~3 columns of lava. Everything else on this level is in motion, so a grid is a
  single frame — this one shows the six orbiting pieces at the moment of the capture.

- Legend extensions used: `_` **lava** (fall hazard, 5 s time penalty), `Q` **empty burner** (no
  cookware), `I` rice crate, `C` chicken crate, `!` tortilla crate. The tiles at (13,0), (9,1),
  (8,3), (0,5), (6,7) and (14,7) are the **orbiting** pieces, not fixtures.
- Station inventory: boards 2 (one per platform), pot burners 0 fixed, pan burners 0 fixed —
  **all three burners orbit** (`S` one with a pot, `F` one with a pan, `Q` one empty), fryers 0,
  ovens 0, mixers 0, steamers 0, **sinks 0**, serve counters 1, plate returns 1, bins 1,
  extinguishers 1, plates 2, crates 4 (rice + beef on the left platform, chicken + tortilla on the
  right), plain counters 11 — of which **3 orbit**. Floor 44, counter 24, station 13.
- Spawns: one chef per platform — (3,2) on the left and (12,2) on the right `(estimate, matching the
  capture)`.
- Zones:
  - **Left platform** (cols 1-6, rows 2-6): rice and beef crates, a chopping board, counters; the
    serving counter and plate return hang off its western edge at (0,3) and (0,4).
  - **Right platform** (cols 10-15, rows 2-6): chicken and tortilla crates, a chopping board, the
    bin and the extinguisher.
  - The two platforms are **never joined**. Everything crosses on the orbiting counters and burners —
    put an item down as one passes, walk away, and collect it on the other side. An expert can also
    **dash along the edges of the moving pieces** to change platform (wiki Strategies).
  - Consequence for the features block: the chicken and tortilla crates have **no walkable route** to
    the serving counter at all; `longestWalkTiles` (4) only measures the rice and beef crates on the
    serve's own platform.

## Dynamics
- **Figure-8 orbit.** Wiki: "3 Countertops and 3 Burners circle around the two platforms in a form of
  the Infinity Symbol (∞), allowing chefs to cook and pass ingredients to each other. One of the
  Burners has a Pan on it, one has a Pot, and one is free." Period, speed and phase are not
  published — one full lap in ~30 s, the six pieces evenly spaced `(estimate)`.
- The wiki's first paragraph also says "There is a Pan on the left Countertops while a Pot is placed
  on the right ones", which reads as a description of the starting frame rather than of extra
  cookware; treat the pan and pot as the ones on the orbiting burners `(ambiguous on the wiki)`.
- **Dead zone**: "Do not leave cooking Ingredients passing behind the Service Counter, as you won't
  have a way to access them, leading to them getting burnt." Part of the orbit passes behind the
  serving counter where no chef can stand — anything left on a burner there is lost.
- **Lava**: falling in costs 5 s.

## Cooperation notes
- One chef per platform is forced, and neither can cook: the burners are all in orbit. So the loop is
  chop on your platform, catch a burner as it passes, load it, and hope to catch it again on the far
  side of the lap.
- The bottleneck is the **orbit period**. A burrito needs rice boiled and meat fried, both on
  orbiting burners, plus a tortilla that lives on the opposite platform — so a single dish spans at
  least two laps.
- The wiki's expert tip is to change platforms by dashing along the edges of the moving pieces, which
  turns the orbit into a ferry.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Orbiting stations on a closed path** — tiles that carry their cookware and the items on them
   around a figure-8 loop, with items burning while they are out of reach. The clone's `sliders`
   move a group on one axis; this needs a waypoint loop and per-tile phase offsets.
2. **Lava / pit tiles with a 5 s fall penalty.**
3. **Empty burners** — a burner tile with no cookware, so that cookware can be placed on and taken
   off it. The clone's `S` and `F` always start with a pot or pan.
4. **Burrito recipe family** and the rice / tortilla / chicken crates.
5. Two clean plates that start **on** the plate return tile, and a sink-less dirty-plate cycle.
6. Dashing along a moving tile's edge as a way to change platform — a physics behaviour, not a
   station.
