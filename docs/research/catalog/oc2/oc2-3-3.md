# oc2 3-3 — Conjurer's Kitchen
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-3_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 3-3.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00 (240 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 20 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Pizza and Pepperoni Pizza, 2 order variants.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~26 s, max 4, ~100 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Three plates, starting on the horizontal middle counters.
- Obstacles: moving tables, isolated chefs (infobox).
- Timing the wiki does give: all four quadrants are open for the first 10 seconds; then the boxes close a vertical split, and the split alternates vertical/horizontal every 30–40 seconds.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 200/280/380/660; 2P 380/480/640/1450; 3P 440/600/800/1650; 4P 440/600/800/1750. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **moving-crates** — the dough, pepperoni, tomato and cheese boxes slide to wall off quadrants. All four quadrants are open for the first 10 s; a vertical split closes next, and vertical and horizontal splits then alternate every 30–40 s. Period 35 s.
- Hazard: oven fire

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki tip that defines the level: keep one clean plate on the middle counter so both chefs can finish a pizza whichever way the walls fall.
- Both recipes need dough and chopped tomato, so the wiki advises over-prepping those two while you can reach them.
- Bottleneck: only one of the two ovens is on each chef's side after a split, and the right-hand oven is easy to miss (the wiki says so outright).

## Recreate checklist
- Moving crate counters that open and close walls on a schedule — the same primitive as 3-1's sliding counter, but four of them on two axes.
- Oven and the pizza recipe; dough, cheese and pepperoni ingredients.
- Throwing between quadrants.
