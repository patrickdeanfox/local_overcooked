Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-5_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 1-5.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 3:00 (180 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 5 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Pasta — Tomato Pasta only, 1 order variant (boiled pasta + chopped-then-fried tomato). First pasta level.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~26 s, max 4, ~100 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Only two plates, starting on the big balloon to the right of the chopping counter.
- Obstacles: shifting platforms and moving tables; infobox `dynamic = Yes`.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 40/100/200/700; 2P 80/180/260/900; 3P 100/240/360/1100; 4P 100/240/360/1200. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **sliders** — the three-board counter on the big balloon slides up and down across the deck; the wiki gives no period.
- **moving-platforms** — the two balloons drift, opening and closing the crossing in the middle of the level; no period on the wiki.
- Hazard: fall off a balloon: chef respawns after a short delay (duration not on the wiki)

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki roles: the small-balloon chef fetches ingredients, washes and delivers; the big-balloon chef fries, boils and chops. At 3–4 players the big balloon splits into fryer, cooker and chopper.
- Bottleneck: two plates only, and both cooked components live on the balloon that has no serve window — every dish has to make the crossing.
- Nothing gets cooked unless the small-balloon chef keeps feeding raw pasta and tomato across.

## Recreate checklist
