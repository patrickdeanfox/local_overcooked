# oc2 1-4 — Sushi City
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-4_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 1-4.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 3:30 (210 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 4 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Fish Sushi and Cucumber Sushi, 2 order variants.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~22 s, max 4, ~80 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Four plates, all starting in the centre of the kitchen.
- Obstacles: conveyor belts and a narrow corridor (infobox). First conveyor level in the game.
- The wiki Overview says the four boxes are "shrimp, nori, cucumber and rice", but neither recipe on the level uses prawn — the blue crate in the screenshot is fish. Treated as a wiki slip; fish is used here.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 80/180/340/950; 2P 120/260/400/1100; 3P 140/280/460/1500; 4P 140/280/460/1600. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **conveyors** — two full-width horizontal belts, the upper one carrying right and the lower one carrying left. Belt speed is not on the wiki.
- Hazard: burnt rice: pots left too long catch fire (no extinguisher is mentioned on the wiki page)

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki strategy: always travel with the belt, dash if you must go against it; the rice cook in particular should ride the flow.
- 2-player split is chopper/plater against rice-cook/dishwasher, and the belts make the loop one-directional so the two chefs rarely meet head-on.
- Bottleneck: three pots at the right and a single serve at the bottom middle, with a two-tile corridor between them.

## Recreate checklist
- Conveyor belts that move chefs and items — the clone has none.
- Cucumber, nori, rice, fish ingredients and the sushi recipe.
- Impassable decorative islands in the middle of the floor (already expressible as `#`).
