# oc1 1-4 — Treacle Town
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-4_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/1-4.jpg, oc1/1-4-ayce.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00, `preptime = No` in Overcooked! (All You Can Eat adds prep on the first playthrough only). Unlock: the Stars to Unlock table says 5; the infobox repeats 1-3's 4. The table is the better source. 1-4 players.
- Burger, Lettuce Burger and Salad Burger — the level that introduces burgers and frying pans, with a scripted visual tutorial for the Meat Burger at the start. Three variants. No cadence on the wiki; the clone runs initial 2, every 20 s, max 4, 100 s each, tightened from 24 s after a playtest showed the ticket supply, not the kitchen, was capping the score.
- The level page's OC1 star columns are blank. The Level 1 summary gives 1P 40 / 90 / 160 and the overview table gives the 2P 3-star of 280; the clone scales the 1P row by 280/160 to [70, 160, 280]. Provenance `oc1` for 1P and for the 2P 3-star, derived for the rest.
- `plates = Yes`: sink (2,0) with its board (3,0), plate return (12,5). **Five clean plates**, the most of any level in the series — (12,6) and (8,7)-(11,7).

## Dynamics
- Nothing moves. `dynamic = No`; the only obstacle is the "1 block pathway through the middle".

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki names the division of labour: "place chopped Beef on the Counters in the middle of the level so that another chef can grab it and fry it". West chops, east fries and plates.
- Second wiki tip, and the reason for five plates: "wash Plates only if there are 3-5 in the Sink/Plate Return" — batching the washing rather than reacting to each dirty plate.
- The bottleneck is the corridor, and the answer is to not use it. The playtest measured 37.8 s for a first salad burger and 22.0 s for the next when the hand-off happens on the divider counters instead.
