# oc2 1-3 — Sushi City
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-3_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 1-3.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 3:00 (180 s). No delayed-timer note on the wiki, so the clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 3 stars. Playable 1–4 players. Earning a 2x tip combo here unlocks the hidden level Kevin 1.
- Recipes: Prawn Sashimi and Cucumber Sushi, 2 order variants.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~22 s, max 4, ~80 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: first level in the game with dirty plates and a sink. Four plates — two on the left island, two on the right counter. Sink and plate return are together on the bottom right.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 40/120/160/890; 2P 40/160/320/1250; 3P 40/200/340/1600; 4P 40/200/340/1900. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

- Grid size: 13 x 9, counted from 1-3.jpg; method: counter seams; the three boards on the top-left run and the three pots on the top-right run are single tiles, and the sink reads as a two-tile unit (draining board + basin); confidence med

- Legend extensions used:
  - `I` rice crate
  - `Ø` prawn crate
  - `!` other crate — nori at (0,3), cucumber at (12,3)
- Station inventory: board 3, potBurner 3, sink 1, drying 1, serve 1, plateReturn 1, trash 1, plate 4, counter 31; crates: rice x1, prawn x1, nori x1, cucumber x1
- Spawns (best guess): (3,6), (9,6)
- Zones:
  - **left half** — 3 boards, rice/prawn/nori crates, serve, 2 plates. → right half via walk (only along the bottom row, past the bin); → right half via throw
  - **right half** — 3 pot burners, cucumber crate, sink, drying, plate return, 2 plates. → left half via walk (bottom row) or throw
- The centre counter column runs from the top wall down to the bin, so the two halves meet only along the bottom row — the wiki calls the level a diagonal and splits chefs left/right.

## Dynamics
- Nothing moves and nothing is scheduled; the infobox lists no obstacles.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki 2-player split: left chef chops, plates and serves and hands raw rice across; right chef washes up, boils rice and hands cucumber across.
- Bottleneck: the one-tile gap under the centre counter — every walked hand-off queues there, which is why the wiki's roles are built around throwing instead.
- At 4 players the wiki assigns chop / plate-serve / rice / dishes as four separate jobs.
