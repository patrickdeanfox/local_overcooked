# oc2 1-1 — Sushi City
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-1_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 1-1.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 2:30 (150 s). The timer does not start until the first dish is served — the wiki Strategies section says so explicitly, because this is the game's first sashimi level.
- Unlock: 1 star. Playable 1–4 players (the Star Chart has a row for each).
- Recipes: Sashimi — Fish Sashimi and Prawn Sashimi, 2 order variants. One chop, no heat.
- Order pattern: the wiki gives none. Estimate: 2 tickets on screen at the start, a new one every ~18 s, at most 4 at once, ~60 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: no sink. Four plates start on the two centre islands (two each) and dirty plates come back clean at the plate return, which sits directly beside the serving counter. The wiki's advice is never to move plates away from the return.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 20/60/240/1300; 2P 20/160/300/2650; 3P 20/200/400/3100; 4P 20/200/400/3300. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- Nothing moves and nothing is scheduled; the infobox lists no obstacles.

## Cooperation notes
- One open room, so cooperation is voluntary: the two crates sit at opposite walls and the two board clusters sit in opposite bottom corners, which naturally splits the room left/right.
- The bottleneck is the single serve tile and the fact that all four plates live on two islands in the middle — everybody converges there.
- Wiki strategy: with 4 chefs, pre-chop a stockpile of fish and prawn (even on the floor) before serving the first dish, because the clock has not started yet.

## Recreate checklist
- New ingredients: fish, prawn (crates and chopped forms).
- New recipe family: sashimi — a chop-only dish that plates straight from the board.
- Clean plate return: dirty plates come back washed at `R` after a delay, with no sink. The clone has `plates.mode: 'sink'` and `'stack'` but no delayed clean return.
- Timer that starts on the first serve is already supported (`timerStartsOnFirstServe`).
