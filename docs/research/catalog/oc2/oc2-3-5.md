# oc2 3-5 — Ravenous Rapids
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-5_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 3-5.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00 (240 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 24 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Chips, Nuggets and Nuggets & Chips — 3 order variants, the full Fast Food list.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~24 s, max 4, ~90 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink, and only **two** plates — the infobox lists "2 plates" as an obstacle in its own right. Both start on the right raft.
- Obstacle: moving platforms (infobox). The wiki says the rafts occasionally drift too far apart to walk between.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 140/180/240/830; 2P 240/300/420/850; 3P 240/320/440/950; 4P 240/320/440/950. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

- Same supplier/cook split as 2-1 but reversed: here the raft with the crates also has the fryers and the serve, and the other raft owns chopping and washing.
- With only two plates the washing raft controls the tempo of the whole level.

## Dynamics
- **moving-platforms** — both rafts drift; the wiki states outright that they are sometimes too far apart to walk between. No period given.
- Hazard: fall in the river: chef respawns (delay not on the wiki)
- Hazard: fryer fire

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki roles: the left chef throws raw potato and chicken across and always throws extra; the right chef chops and washes.
- Bottleneck: two plates and two fryers. Because throwing is the transport layer rather than an optimisation, a missed throw costs a whole dish.
- 3P and 4P thresholds are identical, so extra chefs past three add nothing the level rewards.

## Recreate checklist
- Deep fryer and the Fast Food recipes (all three variants).
- Drifting rafts whose gap opens and closes, with throwing as the fallback transport.
- Chicken and potato ingredients; a level with only two plates.
