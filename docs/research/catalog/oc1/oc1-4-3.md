# oc1 4-3 — Munch Mansion (moving stations)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/4-3_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/4-3.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **38 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 24.
  Player counts 1-4 (Strategies give a 3-chef and a 4-chef plan).
- Recipes: Burger, Lettuce Burger, Salad Burger (`MeatBurger`, `LettuceBurger`,
  `TomatoLettuceBurger`) — 3 order variants. Cadence not published: initial 2, one new order every
  20 s, max 4 on screen, 60 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1**: 3★ only — 90 (1P), 120 (2P), from the
  [Overcooked! Levels](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Overcooked!_Levels) overview table — the
  lowest 3★ bar in world 4. **AYCE**: 80/180/280/440 (1P), 120/260/420/660 (2P),
  180/400/640/1000 (3P), 240/520/800/1240 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 60/120/200/320 (1P),
  120/260/400/640 (2P), 160/340/540/860 (3P), 160/340/540/840 (4P).
- Plates: sink kitchen. 3 clean plates, and they start on the **dividing counter run between the
  quadrants**, which is exactly where the wiki puts them ("Three Plates are placed on Countertops
  between them"). Dirty plates return at the plate return on the north wall.

## Dynamics
- **Relocating stations.** Infobox obstacles: "Moving Chopping Board, Ingredient box and Burners,
  Semi-separated Chefs". Overview: "The kitchen constantly switches the places of the Burners, the
  Beef Ingredient box and the Chopping Board, putting them in between quadrants, blocking off parts
  of the kitchen, while revealing other passageways."
- The wiki's Overview gallery names **five distinct countertop configurations**
  (`oc14-3phase1.jpg` … `oc14-3phase5.jpg`) that the kitchen cycles through. Only the still images
  are on the wiki; the dwell time per configuration is not published — ~25 s each `(estimate)`.
- No fall hazard, no fire source other than burnt pans.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies: "Stay in opposite quadrants (lower-left & top-right or top-left & lower-right) to
  avoid being stuck on one side"; with three chefs the third floats; with four, one per quadrant.
- The bottleneck is the **single chopping board** feeding two pans, in a kitchen where the board
  itself moves. Every burger needs a chopped patty and up to two more chopped salad items, and all of
  them queue at one station.
- The intended feel is that the kitchen, not the players, decides who is separated from whom, so the
  division of labour has to be re-negotiated every configuration change.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Stations that translate between tiles** — the clone's `sliders` move counter groups on one axis
   with a fixed amplitude; here whole stations (burners, a crate, the board) teleport-slide to new
   tiles, changing which quadrants connect. Needs a per-station waypoint schedule.
2. Burgers exist in the clone already (pan, board, crates, sink, plate return, extinguisher), so
   once the moving stations exist this level needs no new recipes or station types.
3. A serving counter two tiles tall and a bin on the same wall — cosmetic, already expressible.
