# oc1 6-3 — Glazed Glacier (two rows of ice floes)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/6-3_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/6-3.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **71 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 50.
  Player counts 1-4. 4-star difficulty "Hard". The wiki describes it as a harder
  [3-4](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-4_(Overcooked!)).
- Recipes: Onion Soup, Tomato Soup, Mushroom Soup — 3 order variants. Cadence not published:
  initial 2, one new order every 22 s, max 4 on screen, 90 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 60/160/260/400 (1P), 120/260/400/620 (2P),
  140/300/480/760 (3P), 160/360/580/900 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 40/80/140/240 (1P),
  40/100/180/300 (2P), 60/140/220/340 (3P), 60/160/260/420 (4P). The JSON carries the AYCE-Switch
  numbers, **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: sink kitchen; the sink is on the **right** bank with the boards. 3 plates, on the right
  bank's south counter beside the tomato crate. Plate return also on the right bank.

## Layout
- Grid size: **18 x 11**, counted from `6-3.jpg`; confidence **low**. Method: the three plates on the
  right bank and the two crates on the left give a ~45 px tile, which makes each bank about 6 columns
  and the river about 6; rows come from the bank height. Confidence is low because the floes are
  irregular in size and shape (the wiki says so explicitly) and because the banks' outer ledges run
  off the framed area.

- Station inventory: boards 2 (right bank, north), **pot burners 2** (left bank, upper section),

  (right bank), plate returns 1 (right bank), bins 1 (left bank, lower section), extinguishers 1
  (on the east end of the left bank's dividing counter), plates 3 (right bank), crates 3 —
  **left bank lower: mushroom and onion; right bank: tomato**, plain counters 27. Floor 109 (89 ice
  bank + 20 floe), counter 43, station 16.
- Spawns: the capture puts both chefs on the left bank, at (2,6) and (4,5) `(estimate)`.
- Zones:
  - **Upper-left bank**: two pots on burners.
  - **Lower-left bank**: bin, onion and mushroom crates. Split from the upper section by the counter
    run at row 4, whose east end carries the extinguisher.
  - **Right bank**: sink, two chopping boards, serving counter, plate return, three plates, tomato
    crate — everything else.
  - The banks are joined only by the **two floe lanes**, and by narrow outer ledges around the top and
    bottom of the map `(the wiki Strategies mention the ledges; they run off the edge of the capture,
    so they are not on the grid)`.
  - In this frame no crate on the left bank has a walkable route to the serving counter: the floes in
    the two lanes are not aligned. That is the level — the crossing only exists when they line up.
- Both banks are **slippery ice**: momentum carries the chef through turns
  ([3-1](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-1_(Overcooked!)) gives the same behaviour).

## Dynamics
- **Two counter-running floe lanes.** Wiki: "There are two rows of floes. The left ones go upwards,
  while the right ones float downwards. Unlike the predecessor of this kitchen, the floes differ in
  shape and sizes and come rather quickly." Speed, spacing and the size distribution are not
  published — a floe every ~4 s per lane, drifting at ~1 tile/s, sizes 1x1 to 2x2 `(estimate)`.
- **Falling in** costs 5 s ([Time Penalty](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-4_(Overcooked!))).
- **Slippery floor** on both banks; the floes themselves are also ice.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies: take a **pot off its burner** and carry it to the right bank, load it there, and
  bring it back rather than ferrying ingredients one at a time; dash before stepping onto a floe and
  line the floes up to clear the river in one go; or skip the river entirely by walking the outer
  ledges.
- The intended division is **left bank cooks, right bank preps**: the two pots are on the left, the
  two boards and the sink on the right, and the serving counter is on the right too. So chopped
  ingredients travel left, and finished soup travels right — two crossings per dish unless you move
  the pot instead.
- The bottleneck is the river. Everything else about the kitchen is generous (two pots, two boards,
  three plates), which is why the AYCE-Switch thresholds are among the lowest in world 6.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Moving platforms over a fall hazard** — floes that carry the chef and any item on them, in two
   lanes running opposite ways, with random sizes and spacing.
2. **Water tiles with a 5 s fall penalty.**
3. **Slippery floor** — momentum-carrying movement on a per-tile surface flag; the clone's chef stops
   instantly.
4. **Carrying a pot off its burner** and using it as a portable container — the clone's pots are
   fixed to their stove tile.
5. Everything else is present: three soups, boards, sink, plate return, bin, extinguisher.
