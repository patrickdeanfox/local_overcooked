# oc1 3-1 — Glazed Glacier
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-1_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/3-1.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00, no prep time. Unlock: the Stars to Unlock table says 20; the infobox says 12. The table is the better source. 1-4 players. The level that introduces Fish and Chips, deep fryers and the slippery floor, and the infobox's 4-star difficulty is Hard.
- Chips, Fried Fish and Fish and Chips — three variants off two ingredients. No cadence on the wiki; `orders` is `(estimate)`: initial 2, every 22 s, max 4, 100 s each. Two ingredients and four fryers means throughput is high but every dish needs a deep fry, so the ticket life is long.
- OC1 star columns blank. 3-star only, from the Overcooked! Levels overview table: 1P 200, 2P 220 — the highest one-player 3-star in worlds 1 to 3. Provenance `oc1`; the lower thresholds are `null`.
- `plates = Yes`: sink on the island's west face at (3,4) with its rack at (3,5), plate return on the north ledge at (5,0). Three clean plates on the island's south row at (7,7)-(9,7); the wiki gives no count, so three is read off the screenshot.

## Dynamics
- **Slippery floor.** The ice carries momentum: a chef who releases the stick keeps sliding, and the wiki's only strategy line is "steer into the corners before you even need to turn". No friction figure is published; treat it as a low-drag movement model with the same top speed `(estimate)`.
- **The sea.** Skidding off the floe costs 5 seconds — the same penalty as falling off a truck in 2-1 or into the river in 3-4, and the one number the infobox does give.
- Nothing on the level moves. `dynamic = No`.

## Cooperation notes
- There is no station split at all — one island, one loop of ice, both chefs anywhere. The division of labour has to be by recipe or by step rather than by place: one chef on fish and one on chips, or one prepping while the other fries and plates.
- The bottleneck is the serving hatch, and it is a design choice rather than an accident. Every other counter is on the island; the hatch and the plate return are on their own ledge to the north, so every serve is a separate skid across ice you cannot brake on.
- The second bottleneck is the plates: three of them, a sink on the far side of the island from the hatch, and a dish that spoils nothing but occupies a plate until it is served.
- The wiki has one strategy line and it is about steering, which says how much of this level is movement rather than logistics.

## Recreate checklist
- Fish and Chips: the `chips`, `fish` and `fish_and_chips` recipes, chopping that descales a fish and cuts a potato into chips, and the two-step prep those need.
- Deep fryers with frying baskets — a fifth cooking device, one basket per item, distinct from a pot and a pan.
- A slippery floor: momentum-carrying movement over a tagged floor material.
- A fall hazard with a 5 s time penalty and a respawn.
- New crates for fish and potato.
