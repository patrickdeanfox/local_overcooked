# oc2 2-2 — Buffet Balloons
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/2-2_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 2-2.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 3:30 (210 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 8 stars. Playable 1–4 players. The level nav lists Kevin 2 as the hidden level reached from here.
- Recipes: Beef Pasta and Fish & Prawn Pasta, 2 order variants. Fish & Prawn Pasta is the longest chain in world 2 (boil + two chops + two fries).
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~26 s, max 4, ~100 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: no sink. Three plates, all on the right balloon (one per side except the left side, which carries the plate return).
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 180/240/340/750; 2P 260/360/480/1050; 3P 340/460/620/1250; 4P 340/460/620/1250. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Layout
- Grid size: 17 x 7, counted from 2-2.jpg; method: the right balloon is a square ring of stations and the wiki lists it side by side, which fixes its perimeter; the two smaller balloons are sized from their station counts. Deck planking gives no tile grid, so depths are estimates; confidence low

- Legend extensions used:
  - `Ø` prawn crate
  - `J` fish crate
  - `!` other crate — pasta at (2,6)
  - `=` the gap between two balloons; walkable only while the middle balloon is level with its neighbour
- Station inventory: board 2, potBurner 2, panBurner 3, serve 1, plateReturn 1, trash 1, extinguisher 1, plate 3, counter 30; crates: beef x1, pasta x1, prawn x1, fish x1
- Spawns (best guess): (2,3), (14,3)
- Zones:
  - **left balloon** — serve, beef crate, pasta crate. → middle balloon via walk-when-close; → right balloon via throw
  - **middle balloon (moves up and down)** — 2 chopping boards, prawn crate, fish crate. → left balloon via walk-when-close; → right balloon via walk-when-close
  - **right balloon** — 2 pot burners, 3 pan burners, trash, extinguisher, plate return, 3 plates. → middle balloon via walk-when-close; → left balloon via throw
- The middle balloon is the only ferry between the two ends and it spends most of its cycle out of reach, which is why the wiki tells you to throw beef and pasta straight to the right balloon.
- Note the asymmetry: the serve window is on the left balloon and every burner is on the right, so a finished dish has to travel the whole level.

## Dynamics
- **moving-platforms** — the middle balloon rides up and down the level, connecting to the left and right balloons only when level with them. Period not on the wiki.
- Hazard: fall off a balloon: chef respawns (delay not on the wiki)
- Hazard: pan/pot fire if food is left cooking

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki strategy: throw beef and pasta to the right balloon rather than wait for the ferry; with 3+ players dedicate one chef to throwing.
- Bottleneck: the middle balloon's travel time, and the fact that plating happens on the right balloon while the serve window is on the left.
- Fish & Prawn Pasta needs two pans at once, which is why the right balloon carries three.

## Recreate checklist

- New ingredients: pasta, prawn, fish. New recipe family: pasta, including the two-pan variant.
