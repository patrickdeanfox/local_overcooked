# oc2 3-4 — Conjurer's Kitchen
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-4_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 3-4.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00 (240 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 22 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Meat, Cheese, Lettuce & Cheese and Tomato & Lettuce Burger — 4 order variants, the widest burger list in the base campaign outside 6-x.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~22 s, max 4, ~90 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Four plates — two start on the left side, two on the right.
- Obstacle: portals (infobox). The left and right sides are separated by an uncrossable rift that only items can cross.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 160/240/300/600; 2P 260/320/440/950; 3P 280/400/540/1100; 4P 280/400/540/1400. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

- Zones:
  - **raised middle** — serve, plate return, 2 portal tiles. → left side via portal; → right side via portal
  - **left side** — 2 chopping boards, 3 pan burners, extinguisher, 2 plates, 1 portal tile. → raised middle via portal; → right side via throw across the rift
  - **right side** — sink, drying, trash, 5 crates (tomato, cheese, beef, bun, lettuce), 2 plates, 1 portal tile. → raised middle via portal; → left side via throw across the rift
- Total role separation: the right chef owns every crate, the sink and the bin but has no heat and no boards; the left chef owns every board and pan but has no crates.
- The rift cannot be walked, so the only way from the crates to the pans is a throw — the middle portals move chefs and plates but do not shortcut the rift.

## Dynamics
- **portals** — two fixed portal pairs joining the raised middle to each side. No timing; they are always on.
- Hazard: pan fire — the extinguisher is on the left side only

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki strategy: throw everything right-to-left, carry clean plates through the middle, assemble on the left, then bring finished burgers to the middle to plate and serve immediately.
- Bottleneck: the two starting plates on the left. Once they are used the level becomes a round trip through the portal for every dish.
- Four burger variants against one serve window means the ticket order, not the cooking, sets the pace.

## Recreate checklist
- Portals as a walkable teleport pair.
- A rift: impassable, non-fatal terrain that only throws cross.
- Throwing; cheese; the three garnished burger variants.
