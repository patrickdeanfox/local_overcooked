# oc1 3-2 — Savoury Seas
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-2_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/3-2.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00, no prep time. Unlock: the Stars to Unlock table says 23; the infobox says 14. The table is the better source. 1-4 players. The infobox's 4-star difficulty is Easy, the lowest in world 3.
- All three soups, three variants. No cadence on the wiki; `orders` is `(estimate)`: initial 2, every 22 s, max 4, 95 s each — close to 1-3, which is the same ship deck with the same recipes.
- OC1 star columns blank. 3-star only, from the overview table: 1P 100, 2P 160. Provenance `oc1`.
- Infobox `plates = No`, so there is no sink and dirty plates never appear; the plate return hands them back clean. The Strategies section makes plate handling the level's whole tip: "keep the plates where every chef can reach them". Three plates, read off the screenshot; the wiki gives no count.

## Dynamics
- Nothing moves. `dynamic = No`, and unlike 1-3 the ship's sway does not shift any counter — the obstacle line is just "Separated Chefs".
- The two open cargo grates are static holes in the deck. They block movement but carry no penalty.

## Cooperation notes
- The Overview describes a rotation rather than a split: the chefs "need to switch between handing Ingredients, serving and cooking to Chopping and bringing plates from the Plate Return". Neither half is self-sufficient, and which half is the prep half changes during the run.
- That is why there are two serving hatches, one per half, and why the crates sit on the divider. Whoever is holding a finished soup serves it on their own side.
- The bottleneck is the plate return: it is on the port side only, and with no sink the plates are a fixed pool of three that both chefs need. Hence the wiki's tip — park them on the divider where either chef can take one.
- The wiki's other tip is the general Overcooked trick and it matters most here: take the pot off the burner and scoop the soup out with a plate, rather than carrying plates to pots.

## Recreate checklist
- Plates that return clean with no sink.
- Two serving counters in one kitchen, and orders that either can satisfy.
- Impassable holes in the floor that are not fall hazards — the clone's `void` already does this; the grate is cosmetic on top of it.
- Nothing else. Three soups, pots, boards, crates and bins all exist.
