# oc2 3-6 — Moreish Mines → Ravenous Rapids
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-6_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 3-6.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 4:10 (250 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 26 stars. Playable 1–4 players. World 3 boss level; infobox `dynamic = Yes, changes after 2:20`.
- Recipes: Beef Burrito and Chicken Burrito, 2 order variants.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~26 s, max 4, ~100 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: no sink. Two plates on the bottom platform's left counter `(estimate on the count — the wiki lists the plate return but not the plate count)`.
- Obstacles: moving platforms, isolated chefs (infobox).
- Wiki slip: the Overview says the middle platform carries "rice and chicken on the top right corner, and the beef and chicken on the bottom left". Burritos need tortilla, so the second "chicken" is read here as tortilla.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 240/300/400/1050; 2P 320/420/560/1600; 3P 400/540/740/1800; 4P 400/540/740/2100. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **moving-platforms** — the middle platform swings across the gap between the top and bottom platforms, and is the only walkable link to either. Period not on the wiki.
- **phase-change** — at 2:20 elapsed (1:50 remaining) the mine floods; the three platforms become rafts on the river. The middle raft vanishes briefly during the change and the three then drift while staying mostly connected.
- Hazard: fall into the mine (and later the river): chef respawns (delay not on the wiki)
- Hazard: pan and pot fire — the extinguisher is on the top platform only

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki roles: the top chef fries meat and serves; the bottom chef boils rice, builds the burrito and sends it up on a plate. With three chefs, one rides the middle platform full time as a thrower.
- Bottleneck: crate access. The middle platform is the only source of every ingredient and it is in reach of each side only part of the time.
- The 2:20 flood changes the job rather than the recipe — the same dish, but suddenly all three platforms are reachable, so the endgame is about throughput not logistics.

## Recreate checklist
- A platform that travels on a fixed path and docks alternately with two others.
- Mid-level phase change (mine → river) that re-links the zones.
- Throwing; tortilla, rice, chicken; the burrito recipe.
- Clean plate return without a sink.
