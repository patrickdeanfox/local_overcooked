# oc2 1-2 — Sushi City
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-2_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 1-2.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 2:30 (150 s). The timer starts on the first serve — the wiki says so, this being the first sushi level.
- Unlock: 2 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Sushi — Fish Sushi only, 1 order variant (nori + boiled rice + chopped fish).
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial tickets, one every ~22 s, max 4, ~80 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: no sink. Four plates — two on the top middle counter, two on the bottom counter. Plate return sits next to the serving window on the top right.
- Obstacle: pedestrians (infobox). They walk through the kitchen and shove chefs.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 60/180/300/1350; 2P 60/220/420/2200; 3P 60/300/460/3100; 4P 60/300/460/2800. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **pedestrians** — townspeople cross the kitchen through gaps in the side walls and block chefs; the wiki names the obstacle but gives no lane, spacing or speed. Lane row and cadence are estimates.
- Hazard: pedestrian collisions: a chef walked into is stopped/shoved (no damage)

## Cooperation notes
- The boiled-rice chain and the chop-fish chain run at different speeds, so the wiki's 2-player split is one rice cook and one plater/server; at 3–4 players nori and fish become their own jobs.
- Bottleneck: the three pots at the bottom right against a single serve tile at the top right — the plate has to travel the diagonal every time.
- Wiki trick: park the working plate on the fish crate so the rice cook can reach it without crossing the room.
