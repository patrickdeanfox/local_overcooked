# oc2 5-3 — Moreish Mines
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-3_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc2/5-3.jpg`

## Facts
- **Time** 4:00 (240 s). Timer starts immediately (the wiki notes no prep time). **Unlock** 50 stars. Supports 1–4 players.
- **Recipes** (3 order variants): Beef Burrito, Chicken Burrito, Mushroom Burrito.
- **Order pattern** — the wiki states none for any OC2 level. `(estimate)`: 2 initial tickets, one new ticket every 20 s, at most 5 on screen, 80 s on each ticket. Derived from the time limit and the recipe length, not observed.
- **Stars** (1★/2★/3★, from the page's Star Chart; provenance `oc2` base game): 1P 120/140/200 · 2P 280/360/480 · 3P 300/360/500 · 4P 300/360/500. The 4★ bonus tier is 680 / 1050 / 1050 / 1050. The 1-player 1★–3★ (120/140/200) is the lowest bar in worlds 4–6.
- **Plates** `sink` — the level has a sink and plates come back dirty at the plate return. 4 plates. The wiki is explicit: the four plates start one at each corner of the station ring. The plate return and the sink are on opposite walls (right and top-left), so plate laps go all the way round the pit.

- **Legend extensions used**
  - `_` — the pit the platform floats in — everything except the platform is a fall
  - `=` — the steerable platform the chefs stand on
  - `?` — the joystick in the middle of the platform
  - `I` — rice crate
  - `C` — chicken crate
  - `!` — tortilla crate (0,5)
- **Station inventory** (counted from the grid above): 3 boards; 3 burners with pots; 3 burners with pans; 1 sink; 1 serve counter; 1 plate return; 1 bin; 1 extinguisher; 4 plates; 15 plain counters; crates: beef, chicken, mushroom, rice, tortilla.
  None of: deep fryers, ovens, mixers, steamers.
- **Spawns** (best guess, the wiki never states them): (4,3), (5,4).
- **Zones**
  - **steerable platform** — joystick. → station ring via drive the platform to the wall you want
  - **station ring** — potBurner x3, panBurner x3, board x3, sink, serve, plateReturn, trash, extinguisher, plate x4, 5 crates. → steerable platform via reach across from the platform
- `longestWalkTiles` is `null` on purpose: no crate is reachable on foot at all, because the only floor is the platform and it has to be driven to each wall in turn.

## Dynamics
- **steerable-platform** (period unknown) — the whole kitchen floor is one platform the chefs drive around a pit ringed with stations; you cannot be at two walls at once.
- **Hazards** — pit around the platform.

## Cooperation notes
- The whole level is a shared-vehicle problem: you cannot be at the boards and the pots at once, and neither can your partner, because you are standing on the same moving floor.
- The wiki's routine: cook rice in two pots, grab the ingredients and two plates and two tortillas, drive to the boards, chop, drive to the pans, fry, drive back to the pots to plate the rice, then to the serve counter.
- It explicitly recommends running two orders at a time rather than three, and using pan-waiting time to start the next rice or to wash.
