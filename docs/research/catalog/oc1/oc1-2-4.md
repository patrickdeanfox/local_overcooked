## Facts
- 4:00, no prep time. Unlock: the Stars to Unlock table says 17; the infobox says 10. The table is the better source. 1-4 players. The level that introduces conveyors that end in a bin.
- The three burgers, three variants. No cadence on the wiki; `orders` is `(estimate)`: initial 2, every 24 s, max 4, 110 s each — a shade faster than 2-3 because the belts here are short hops rather than a lap of the room.
- OC1 star columns blank. 3-star only, from the overview table: 1P 110, 2P 160 — the softest pair of numbers in worlds 2 and 3. Provenance `oc1`.
- `plates = Yes`, and the Overview gives the count outright: **3 plates**, with the chopping stations, the serving counter and the plate return, all on the north chef's side. The sink is on the south chef's side, so a dirty plate has to cross the divider to be washed.

## Dynamics
- Belts that terminate in a bin rather than looping. "The Conveyor Belts not circling around the kitchen, rather leading to Trash Bins." Whatever you send and nobody catches is destroyed.
- Belt speed is not on the wiki: `(estimate)` 1.5 tiles/s, the same as 2-3, which gives a chef about four seconds to catch an item on a six-tile run before it reaches a bin.
- Cookware sent into a bin comes back: the [Trash Bin](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Trash_Bin) page says trashed items respawn after **5 seconds** at the place they were initially found, clean plates still clean and dirty ones still dirty. That number is a wiki fact.
- Nothing else moves; `dynamic = No`.

## Cooperation notes
- The Overview reads 2-4 as 2-3 with two changes: three pans instead of two, and the belts feed bins instead of a loop. The split is the same shape — one chef has the crates, pans, sink and extinguisher, the other has the boards, plates, serving counter and plate return.
- The change is what it costs to be out of position. In 2-3 an unattended item goes round the loop and comes back. Here it goes in the bin. So the two chefs have to be synchronised rather than merely busy, and the level rewards calling out what you are sending.
- The extra pan is the compensation: three pans against two means the cooking side can absorb a batch of patties in one go, which suits sending several at once.
- The level has no strategy section on the wiki.

## Recreate checklist
- Conveyor belts, as for 2-3.
- A belt whose end tile is a bin, and the 5 s cookware respawn that goes with it.
- Nothing else.
