# oc1 6-2 — Treacle Town (the level with two layouts)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/6-2_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/6-2.jpg` (1-3 players), `docs/research/screens/oc1/6-2-4p.jpg` (4 players)

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **68 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 47.
  Player counts 1-4 — and this is "the only [level] in the entire series to have completely different
  layouts for different numbers of players, not only small changes such as removing or adding
  Countertops".
- Recipes: Onion Soup, Tomato Soup, Mushroom Soup — 3 order variants. Cadence not published:
  initial 2, one new order every 18 s, max 4 on screen, 60 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 120/260/400/640 (1P), 220/500/780/1200 (2P),
  260/580/920/1420 (3P), 160/360/580/920 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 80/180/280/460 (1P),
  180/400/620/960 (2P), 220/480/760/1180 (3P), 160/360/580/920 (4P). The JSON carries the
  AYCE-Switch numbers, **not OC1 values**. Note the 4P thresholds are *lower* than the 3P ones — the
  four-quadrant layout is harder than the two-kitchen one.
- Plates: sink kitchen in both layouts. **4 plates, 2 per kitchen** (1-3P) or 2 per serving quadrant
  (4P). Each kitchen/serving quadrant has its own plate return.

## Layout
- Grid size: **13 x 12**, counted from `6-2.jpg`; confidence **med**. Method: the north counter run
  reads as about 11 tiles at ~54 px, which with the two brick walls gives 13 columns; the west wall's
  station stack (2 pot burners, serving counter, plate return, 2 plates) plus the floor bands gives
  12 rows. The right kitchen is a **180° rotation** of the left, which cross-checks the count.

- Station inventory (1-3P): boards 2 (one per kitchen), **pot burners 4** (two per kitchen),
  pan burners 0, fryers 0, ovens 0, mixers 0, steamers 0, sinks 2, serve counters 2 (2 tiles each),
  plate returns 2, bins 4, extinguishers 0 visible in the capture, plates 4, conveyor tiles 10,
  crates 4 — **left kitchen: tomato + onion; right kitchen: onion + mushroom**, plain counters 24.
  Floor 94, counter 62, station 38.
- Spawns: the capture puts one chef in each kitchen, at (3,8) and (9,8) `(estimate)`.
- Zones: **two self-sufficient kitchens**, mirror-rotated, that never touch. The only thing between
  them is the conveyor column, and it runs into a bin, so anything sent across and not caught is
  destroyed. The wiki's verdict: trading is "pointless".

### 4 player layout (`6-2-4p.jpg`)
The same outer shell, but a **second conveyor runs east-west across the middle**, making a cross that
cuts the kitchen into four quadrants, one chef each. Reading the capture:
- **Upper-left**: all three ingredient crates (tomato, onion, mushroom) on its east counter column,
  one pot on a burner, and the sink on the north wall.
- **Lower-right**: the same set, rotated — three crates, one pot burner, sink on the south wall.
- **Lower-left** and **upper-right**: serving counter, plate return, two plates and a chopping board
  each.
- The conveyors run **clockwise** and all of them end in bins, so passing is now compulsory and
  every hand-off is timed.

**Correction.** The wiki's 4-player section says "the lower-left and the upper-right quadrants have
all of the Ingredient Boxes, a single Pot on a Burner and the Sink, while the upper-left and the
lower-right have Service Counters, Plate Returns, Chopping Boards and two Plates." The capture shows
the **opposite pairing**: crates and sink in the upper-left and lower-right, serving counters in the
lower-left and upper-right. The screenshot wins; the wiki has the diagonal the wrong way round.

## Dynamics
- **Conveyor belts into bins.** 1-3P: one belt down the centre. 4P: a clockwise ring, all terminating
  in bins. Belt speed is not published — ~1.5 tiles/s `(estimate)`. Per
  [Trash Bin](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Trash_Bin), cookware binned this way respawns after
  5 s where it started.
- Nothing else moves. No hazards.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies (1-3P): "Have one side work tomato soup, the other mushroom soup, and split the
  onion soup making." Since both kitchens have onion, the onion orders are the only shared work, and
  they are shared by *duplication*, not by trading.
- Wiki Strategies (4P): "Have one half work separately from the other and coordinate the halves!"
- The bottleneck differs by layout. At 1-3 players there is no bottleneck at all beyond raw speed —
  each chef has a complete kitchen, and the third chef simply joins the left one. At 4 players the
  bottleneck is the conveyor ring: chopped ingredients and clean plates have to circulate, and every
  missed catch is binned.
- This is the level to copy if the clone ever wants **player-count-dependent layouts**, because it is
  the only one in the series that has them.
