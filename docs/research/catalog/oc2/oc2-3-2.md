# oc2 3-2 — Conjurer's Kitchen
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/3-2_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 3-2.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00 (240 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 18 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Meat Burger, Cheese Burger and Lettuce & Cheese Burger, 3 order variants.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~22 s, max 4, ~90 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: no sink. Four plates on the middle section's top counter.
- Obstacles: moving staircases, portals (infobox). First portal level.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 160/240/320/750; 2P 300/360/500/950; 3P 340/480/640/1250; 4P 340/480/640/1350. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

## Dynamics
- **moving-stairs** — the staircase into the sunken middle starts on the left side and swaps sides every 35 seconds. Period 35 s.
- **portals** — two fixed portal pairs: the lower pair walks chefs between the left and right tiers, the upper pair carries thrown items.
- Hazard: pan fire — the extinguisher is on the right tier only
- Hazard: falling into the sunken middle from a tier (assumed; the wiki does not say)

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki strategy: keep buns already on plates in the centre; one chef chops on the left and throws everything down, the other parks in the middle frying patties.
- Bottleneck: the middle holds all four pans and all four plates but no serve window, so every finished burger must reach the right tier — by stairs when they are there, by portal otherwise.
- Because the stairs move on a 35 s timer and the portals do not, the reliable route is always the portal; the stairs are the optimisation.

## Recreate checklist
- Portals: a paired tile that teleports a chef, and a second pair that only carries thrown items.
- Stairs that relocate on a timer, i.e. an edge in the walk graph that appears and disappears — close to the clone's `gate` dynamic but with two alternating positions.
- New ingredients: bun, cheese, lettuce (lettuce exists). Burger variants beyond the plain one.
- Clean plate return without a sink.
