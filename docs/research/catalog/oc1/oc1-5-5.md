# oc1 5-5 — Cosmic Canteen (two shuttling side chambers)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-5_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/5-5.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **57 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 38.
  Player counts 1-4.
- Recipes: Onion Soup, Tomato Soup, Mushroom Soup — 3 order variants. Cadence not published:
  initial 2, one new order every 20 s, max 4 on screen, 75 s each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 40/100/180/280 (1P), 60/160/260/420 (2P),
  120/280/440/700 (3P), 100/220/340/540 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 20/60/120/200 (1P),
  80/180/280/440 (2P), 100/220/360/560 (3P), 100/220/360/580 (4P). These are among the **lowest
  thresholds in the game** — 20 points (one soup) for 1★ at one player. The JSON carries the
  AYCE-Switch numbers, **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: sink kitchen; the sink is on the north wall in the upper half. **3 plates, and they start
  in the lower half** on the south wall — the opposite side from the sink and the plate return, which
  is a deliberate cruelty.

## Layout
- Grid size: **20 x 11**, counted from `5-5.jpg`; confidence **med**. Method: the central block's
  middle run reads cleanly as 8 tiles across at ~47.5 px each (counter, pot burner, counter,
  mushroom, tomato, onion, pot burner, extinguisher on the north face), and the north wall reads as
  8 tiles too, which fixes the central area at 8 columns; each side chamber is 6 columns wide. Rows
  come from the three floor bands plus the wall and divider rows. The chambers are drawn here in
  their **upper dock**; when sent down they occupy rows 6-9 instead.

- Legend extensions used: `?` **button** (green on the side the chamber is on, red on the other;
  pressing it calls/sends the chamber), `G` the **chamber doorway** — walkable only when a chamber is
  docked on that side.
- Station inventory: boards 2 (both on the divider's south face, facing the lower half),
  **pot burners 2** (both on the divider's north face, facing the upper half), pan burners 0,
  fryers 0, ovens 0, mixers 0, steamers 0, sinks 1, serve counters 1, plate returns 1,
  **bins 2** (one in each shuttle chamber), extinguishers 1, plates 3, buttons 4, crates 3
  (mushroom, tomato, onion — all on the divider's north face), plain counters 45. Floor 82,
  counter 66, station 21.
- Spawns: one chef per half — (9,2) above and (9,7) below `(estimate, matching the capture)`.
- Zones:
  - **Upper half** (cols 6-13, rows 1-3): serving counter, plate return, sink, two green buttons; and
    from its south edge the two pot burners, the three crates and the extinguisher on the divider.
  - **Lower half** (cols 6-13, rows 6-9): the two chopping boards on the divider's south face, three
    plates on the south wall, two red buttons, and a lot of empty counter.
  - **Left chamber** and **right chamber** (cols 1-4 and 15-18): counters and a bin each. They dock
    against whichever half their button was last pressed from.
  - Upper and lower halves are **permanently separated** by the two-row divider. Every ingredient and
    every plate crosses either over that divider's counters or inside a chamber.
  - `longestWalkTiles` is only 3 because all three crates sit on the divider next to the serving
    counter; the level's real distance is the *chopping* round trip, which cannot be walked at all.

## Dynamics
- **Two shuttling chambers on buttons.** Wiki: "two side chambers that can be transferred between the
  two sides of the main area by pressing buttons on corresponding sides. There are Countertops and
  Trash Bin inside both chambers." Travel time and whether a chef can ride along are not published —
  ~4 s per trip, chefs ride `(estimate, by analogy with 5-1 where the shuttle is explicitly walkable)`.
- Nothing else moves. No fall hazard, no conveyor, no timed door.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies, and it is the entire level: "Have one chamber always full of Ingredients and one
  full of Chopped ones. Send the two chambers back and forth, and refill when necessary."
- The intended division is **upper = cook and serve, lower = chop**. The upper chef pulls onions,
  tomatoes and mushrooms from the divider crates, loads a chamber, sends it down; the lower chef
  chops, loads the other chamber, sends it up; the upper chef fills the pots and serves.
- The bottleneck is chamber turnaround, and secondarily the plates: three plates start in the lower
  half but the sink and plate return are in the upper half, so clean crockery is itself a chamber
  cargo.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Buttons and moving rooms** (shared with 5-1) — here there are two rooms and four buttons, and
   each room has two docks.
2. **A doorway tile that is only walkable while a room is docked** — the clone's `gate` is close but
   its schedule is time-based, not tied to a moving room.
3. **Bins that ride inside a moving room.**
4. Everything else is already in the clone: three soups, two pot burners, two boards, a sink, a plate
   return, a serving counter and an extinguisher.
