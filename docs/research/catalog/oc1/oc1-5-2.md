# oc1 5-2 — Melting Magma (four quadrants over lava)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-2_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/5-2.jpg` (genuine OC1 capture), `docs/research/screens/oc1/5-2-ayce.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. Timer starts immediately. Unlock: **47 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 30.
  Player counts 1-4.
- Recipes: Beef Burrito, Chicken Burrito — 2 order variants. The OC1 capture shows the ticket rail
  with **five burrito orders queued at once**, so `orders.max` is at least 5 here, higher than the
  4 this catalog assumes elsewhere. Cadence: initial 3, one new order every 20 s, 75 s each
  `(estimate)`; `max: 5` is read off the screenshot.
- Stars. **OC1: none published.** **AYCE**: 60/160/260/300 (1P), 160/340/540/840 (2P),
  180/400/620/980 (3P), 200/440/700/1080 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 80/180/280/460 (1P),
  100/220/360/560 (2P), 160/360/580/920 (3P), 140/320/520/800 (4P). The JSON carries the
  AYCE-Switch numbers, **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: sink kitchen, sink in the lower-left quadrant with the crates. 2 plates, both in the
  upper-right quadrant with the pans. Plate return in the lower-right beside the serving counter.

## Layout
- Grid size: **13 x 11**, counted from `5-2.jpg`; confidence **low**. Method: the four ingredient
  boxes along the lower-left run set a ~100 px tile in the 1920-wide capture, which makes each
  quadrant about 5 x 4 rock tiles; the lava between them is organic, not tile-aligned, so the exact
  outline of every island is a judgement call. Station membership per quadrant is certain — the wiki
  Overview lists all four.

- Legend extensions used: `_` **lava** (fall hazard, 5 s time penalty), `=` **rock bridge**,
  `I` rice crate, `C` chicken crate, `!` tortilla crate.
- Station inventory: boards 1, pot burners 1, **pan burners 2**, fryers 0, ovens 0, mixers 0,
  steamers 0, sinks 1, serve counters 1, plate returns 1, bins 1, extinguishers 1, plates 2,
  bridges 4, crates 4 (beef, chicken, rice, tortilla), plain counters 5. Floor 62 (58 rock + 4
  bridge), counter 21, station 16. **Counter space is the tightest in the game** — five plain
  counters for a four-quadrant kitchen.
- Spawns: the capture has chefs standing on the upper-left island at (5,3) and on the lower-left at
  (4,7) `(estimate)`.
- Zones:
  - **Upper-left**: fire extinguisher, pot burner, one counter, chopping board.
  - **Upper-right**: bin, counters, two plates, two pan burners.
  - **Lower-left**: sink and every ingredient crate.
  - **Lower-right**: serving counter and plate return — and nothing else.
  - The four are joined in a ring by four bridges — (6,2) top, (9,5) right, (6,7) bottom, (3,5)
    left. **Only three are up at any moment**, so exactly one pair of neighbours is always cut.
    Items cross by walking a bridge or, in OC1 only, by dashing the vertical gaps.

## Dynamics
- **Shifting bridges.** Wiki: "Only 3 out of 4 bridges are active at once, and when they change, one
  falls into the lava, and the other rises." Rotation period and order are not published — a swap
  every ~15 s, cycling around the ring `(estimate)`.
- **Cannon.** "A cannon is present shooting down the middle of the kitchen, acting as an additional
  threat to chefs." The OC1 capture shows three cannonballs in flight down the vertical centre line
  (col 6), spaced about a third of the screen apart. Fire rate not published — one ball every ~3 s,
  travelling top to bottom `(estimate)`; a hit knocks the chef down and drops what they carry
  `(estimate — the wiki does not say what a hit does)`.
- **Lava**: falling in costs **5 seconds** ([Time Penalty](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/2-1_(Overcooked!))
  gives the same 5 s figure used for every OC1 fall).
- OC1-only: "it is possible to dash over the vertical gaps between quadrants if it is timed well
  enough" — a movement exploit the remaster removed.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies: throw the fire extinguisher off its counter to free the tile ("Due to the small
  amount of Countertops"); and "do not walk multiple chefs through the same bridge at the same time"
  because collisions push people into the lava.
- Division of labour follows the ring: whoever is on the crate island preps and passes clockwise
  toward the pans, whoever is near the pans cooks and carries plates to the lower-right.
- The bottleneck is **counter space and bridge timing together**. A burrito needs three ingredients
  from one island, two cooking devices on two other islands, and a plate from a fourth, so every dish
  crosses at least three bridges. The 4-star rating is only "Medium" because the star thresholds are
  set low to compensate.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Lava / pit tiles with a fall penalty** — a non-walkable, non-counter tile that costs the chef
   5 s and respawns them. The clone has no fall hazard at all.
2. **Bridges that raise and lower on a schedule** — the clone's `gate` dynamic (walkable for
   `openSec` of every `periodSec`) is close; it needs a "3 of 4 open" group rule rather than
   independent timers.
3. **A cannon**: a projectile crossing the kitchen on a timer and knocking chefs over.
4. **Burrito recipe family** and the rice / tortilla / chicken crates (shared with 5-1).
5. Throwable stations — the extinguisher-off-its-counter trick needs items to be thrown onto the
   floor and stay there.
