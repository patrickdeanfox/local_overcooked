# oc1 5-1 — Cosmic Canteen (button-driven shuttle chamber)

Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/5-1_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: `docs/research/screens/oc1/5-1.jpg`

## Facts
- Time limit 4:00. **No prep time in Overcooked! 1** — the infobox reads
  `preptime=No (Overcooked!) Yes (Overcooked: All You Can Eat)`, so the timer starts immediately in
  the original and only the remaster adds prep. Unlock: **44 stars**
  ([Stars to Unlock](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_to_Unlock)); the infobox says 28.
  Player counts 1-4; the infobox flags "Separated Chefs (1-2 Players)", so with 3-4 chefs each
  chamber gets more than one cook.
- Recipes: Beef Burrito, Chicken Burrito — 2 order variants. This level **introduces the burrito**
  and the button. Cadence not published: initial 2, one new order every 22 s, max 4 on screen, 75 s
  each `(estimate)`.
- Stars. **OC1: none published** — the level page's OC1 columns are blank and the
  [Overcooked! Levels](https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/Overcooked!_Levels) overview table stops
  at 4-4. **AYCE**: 140/320/520/820 (1P), 180/400/620/980 (2P), 200/500/780/1200 (3P),
  220/500/780/1220 (4P). **AYCE Switch**: 120/260/400/640 (1P), 140/320/520/820 (2P),
  180/400/640/1000 (3P), 240/520/800/1260 (4P). The JSON carries the AYCE-Switch numbers because
  that is what `docs/research/oc1-levels.md` uses for world 5; they are **not OC1 values**.
- Plates: sink kitchen, but the **sink rides in the shuttle**, so a half with the shuttle away
  cannot wash. 4 plates in total, 2 per cooking chamber, on the wall beside the burners. Each
  chamber has its own plate return.

  serve counters 2 (2 tiles each), plate returns 2, bins 1 (in the shuttle), extinguishers 2,
  plates 4, buttons 2, crates 4 (rice, tortilla, chicken, beef — all in the shuttle), plain
  counters 39. Floor 78, counter 66, station 27.
- Spawns: one chef per cooking chamber — (2,2) on the left and (16,3) on the right `(estimate,
  matching the capture)`.
- Zones:
  - **Left cooking chamber** (cols 1-8, rows 1-4): pot burner, pan burner, board, extinguisher, two
    plates, serving counter, plate return, green button.
  - **Right cooking chamber** (cols 12-19, rows 1-4): the same set, mirrored, with the red button.
  - **Shuttle chamber** (cols 1-6, rows 6-7): every ingredient crate, the sink and the bin. Docks
    against one chamber at a time through a 2-tile doorway (here (3,5) and (4,5)).
  - The two cooking chambers are **never connected to each other**. Everything crosses inside the
    shuttle: you load it, press the button, and it carries the goods (and any chef standing in it)
    to the other side.

## Dynamics
- **Shuttle chamber on buttons.** Wiki: "A moving chamber controlled with buttons is used to provide
  Ingredient Boxes, the Sink and the Trash Bin for both chefs. The button that is green (which is on
  the side the chamber is on, the other is always red) will send the chamber to the other side."
  Travel time is not published — ~4 s door-to-door `(estimate)`; the button is a one-shot trigger,
  not a hold, and the shuttle only accepts a press from the side it is docked at `(estimate)`.
- No fall hazard, no timed obstacle, no fire beyond burnt cookware.

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki Strategies: "Bring in 3 Ingredients needed to make a Burrito and then send the chamber over to
  the other side" and "If you have both plates dirty, clean them in the Sink" — i.e. the shuttle is a
  batching problem, and you must remember to keep it long enough to wash up.
- The bottleneck is shuttle round-trips. Each burrito needs rice (boiled), a tortilla and a chopped
  and fried meat, and every one of those starts inside the shuttle, so the number of crossings per
  minute caps the kitchen's throughput regardless of how many burners are free.
- With 1-2 players the chefs are separated by construction; the shuttle is the only way anyone
  changes side, and riding it costs you your own chamber's burners.

## Recreate checklist
1. **Buttons** — a floor or wall trigger that starts a scheduled move. Nothing in the clone reacts to
   a chef's input except stations.
2. **A moving room**: a rectangular block of tiles (floor plus stations plus whatever is standing on
   them, chefs included) that translates between two docks and joins to whichever it is at.
3. **Burrito recipe family**: rice boiled in a pot, meat chopped then fried in a pan, an unprepped
   tortilla, all three plated. New crates: rice, tortilla, chicken.
4. Two serving counters and two plate returns in one level; a sink that is only sometimes reachable.
