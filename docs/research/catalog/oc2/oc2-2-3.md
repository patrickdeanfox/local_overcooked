# oc2 2-3 — Buffet Balloons
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/2-3_(Overcooked!_2) · Screenshots: 2-3.jpg

## Facts
- Time limit 3:30 (210 s). Clock runs from the start.
- Unlock: 9 stars. Playable 1–4 players.
- Recipes: Beef Pasta and Mushroom Pasta, 2 order variants.
- Order pattern: not on the wiki. Estimate: 2 initial, one every ~26 s, max 4, ~100 s per ticket `(estimate)`.
- Plates: sink. Four plates, all on the right balloon.
- Note the 3P and 4P 4★ thresholds (700) are *lower* than the 2P one (750) — transcribed from the wiki Star Chart as given.
- Star thresholds (1★/2★/3★/4★): 1P 120/180/260/700; 2P 220/300/400/750; 3P 260/360/480/700; 4P 260/360/480/700. Provenance: oc2 (wiki Star Chart).

- Zones:
  - **left balloon** — sink, drying, plate return, serve, 1 chopping board, pasta/mushroom/beef crates. → ferry balloon via walk-when-close; → right balloon via throw
  - **right balloon** — 1 chopping board, trash, 4 plates, 2 pan burners, 2 pot burners, extinguisher. → ferry balloon via walk-when-close; → left balloon via throw
  - **ferry balloon** — 4 countertops. → left balloon via moving platform; → right balloon via moving platform
- Plates cannot be thrown, so the ferry balloon is the plate channel and throwing is the ingredient channel — two separate transport systems, which is the level's whole idea.
- All three crates and the sink are on the left; all the heat is on the right.

## Dynamics
- **moving-platforms** — the fourth balloon, carrying four countertops, drifts back and forth beneath the two main balloons. Period not on the wiki.
- Hazard: fall off a balloon: chef respawns (delay not on the wiki)
- Hazard: pan/pot fire

## Cooperation notes
- Wiki roles: left chef chops mushrooms and beef and throws them across, washes plates and puts the clean ones on the ferry; right chef boils, fries and plates.
- Wiki tip: keep the plates on the ferry balloon most of the time so both chefs can reach them.
- Bottleneck: the ferry's travel time, because the finished dish has to come back to the left balloon's serve window.
