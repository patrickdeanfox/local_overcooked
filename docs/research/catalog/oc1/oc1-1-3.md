# oc1 1-3 — Savoury Seas
Source: https://overcooked.fandom.com/wiki/1-3_(Overcooked!) · Screenshots: oc1/1-3.jpg, oc1/1-3-ayce.jpg

## Facts
- 4:00, no prep time. Unlock: 4 stars (infobox and table agree). 1-4 players.
- All three soups — the level that introduces Mushroom Soup. Three variants. No cadence on the wiki; the clone runs initial 2, every 20 s, max 4, 85 s each.
- The level page's OC1 star columns are **blank**. Only the 3-star survives, from the Overcooked! Levels overview table: 1P 100, 2P 200. The clone fills the lower two at 30% and 60% of the 3-star — 1P [30, 60, 100], 2P [60, 120, 200] — which sits inside the band the filled-in OC1 rows elsewhere in world 1 use. Provenance `oc1` for the 3-star, derived for the rest. The AYCE and AYCE-Switch columns on the same page are far higher and are not OC1 numbers.
- Infobox `plates = No` and the Overview says outright "There is no Sink in this kitchen": clean plates respawn at the plate stack, three of them, on the west counter at (1,2)-(1,4).

## Dynamics
- "The middle countertops slide back and forth as the Ship sways in opposite directions." The wiki gives no period or amplitude, so both are `(estimate)`.
- The clone splits the five divider tiles 3 + 2 into groups sliding along y at amplitude 1 and period 6 s in opposite phase, which opens and closes gaps rather than shuffling surfaces sideways. A still cannot settle the axis — both screenshots catch the divider aligned — so this is reasoned from what makes "Moving Counters" an obstacle at all.
- Known artefact of that choice: at amplitude 1 in opposite phase the two groups overlap by up to 1.6 tiles, so the divider is five tiles long only at offset 0.

## Cooperation notes
- The wiki splits the roles for you: "the right half has a Serving Counter, the Plate Return and Ingredient boxes while the left has Chopping Stations and Pots with Burners". So the starboard chef fetches and serves, the port chef chops and cooks, and raw ingredients cross the divider whenever the sway opens a gap.
- Two further wiki tips shape the play: the bottom is not blocked off, so a stubborn chef can walk round instead of waiting for the counters; and the serving counter can be used from the back side, which means the port chef can serve without crossing at all.
- The bottleneck is the plate stack. Three plates, no sink, and three soups in circulation means a soup with nowhere to go blocks a pot.
